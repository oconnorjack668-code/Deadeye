# Rim detector: data and model credits

The rim detector is trained on these public datasets from Roboflow Universe. CC BY 4.0 requires credit
wherever the model is used, so this list ships with it and is linked from the app's credits.

| Dataset | Licence | Source | Images | What we use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ballllls | CC BY 4.0 | https://universe.roboflow.com/lg-8eqkn/ballllls | 1,534 | ball, rim |
| X-Basket | CC BY 4.0 | https://universe.roboflow.com/x-qfelz (X-Basket; exact project link to confirm) | 974 | ball, rim, board |
| basketball-computer-vision | Public domain (CC0) | Roboflow Universe, link to confirm | 235 | ball, rim |
| Basketball Shot Tracking | CC BY 4.0 (checked on its page by the founder, 3 Nov 2026) | Roboflow Universe, link to confirm | 311 | ball, rim, backboard |

Exported by the founder on 3 Nov 2026 in COCO format. Players, referees, scores and ballllls'
separate "basket" boxes are not used.

The links marked "to confirm" must be filled in before the model ships in the app.

Model: RF-DETR (Roboflow), Nano size, Apache 2.0. https://github.com/roboflow/rf-detr
