# Player outreach: gold-standard examples

Examples of the standard to aim at. Communities and numbers are made up;
never send these.

## Example 1: a community post (gives first)

```
Route: a basketball training subreddit (about 40k members, self-promotion
allowed in the weekly thread only, rule 4: link)
Where: the weekly self-promotion thread, not a new post
Tracking: deadeyeapp.vercel.app/?ref=reddit-training

Free 20-minute shooting workout I use (no app needed):
- 5 spots, 10 shots each, catch and shoot
- Then 5 free throws after every spot, tired legs on purpose
- Write down makes per spot; the weakest spot is next week's focus

I'm building a free app that counts makes and misses with your phone
camera so you don't have to write it down. It's early and I'd love
honest feedback on what's confusing: <link>
```

Why it is good: follows the community's rule (weekly thread), useful with
or without the app, honest about being early, one link with a route tag.

## Example 2: email to an adult creator (founder sends)

```
To: the creator's public business email (founder adds it)
Subject: Free shooting tracker for your 3-spot drill videos

Hi,

I'm the founder of Deadeye, a free basketball app built in Wexford. Your
3-spot shooting drill videos are the kind of thing our players already
copy.

Deadeye counts makes and misses with the phone camera and turns a session
into a card players can share. If you'd like to try it on one of your
drills, I'd love to hear what you think. No obligation to post.

If you ever did post about it and we'd given you anything, it would need
an "ad" label, and we'd make sure of that.

Thanks,
<founder>
```

Why it is good: specific to the creator's content, a small ask, no
pressure, disclosure raised up front, adult business contact only.

## Example 3: a route review line

```
| reddit-training | community | weekly thread | live | ref=reddit-training |
  14 visits, 3 sign-ups, 1 first session | keep: 1 active from one post,
  try a challenge post next week | 2026-11-18 |
```

Why it is good: judged on the first session, not the visits; one clear
next step; a hint, not a result, at these numbers (and the line says so in
the plan).
