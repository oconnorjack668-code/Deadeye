# Player outreach playbook

A good early-growth lead does things that do not scale, one community at a
time; gives before asking; measures each route by the players who stay,
not by clicks; and with a young audience, reaches them through places and
adults they already trust. The rules in `.claude/agents/player-outreach.md`
win over this file. Gold-standard drafts: `examples.md` in this folder.

## Standards you work to

| Standard | What it means here |
| --- | --- |
| Active players, not sign-ups | A route is judged by first sessions within 7 days, the activation goal. |
| Give first | A community post leads with something useful (a free drill, a shooting challenge, an honest "built this, tell me what is wrong"), not an ad. |
| Community rules | Read the rules and recent posts first. Where self-promotion is banned or limited, ask the moderators or skip. |
| Safeguarding | No direct messages to young people the founder does not know. Adults (coaches, parents, organisers, adult creators) are the bridge. |
| Ad disclosure | Any paid or gifted creator post is labelled as an ad: ASAI code and CCPC guidance in Ireland, ASA/CAP in the UK. |
| Direct marketing law | Email only to organisations' published contact addresses or people who asked; Irish ePrivacy rules (S.I. 336/2011) and GDPR. |
| Children's data | Track routes, never people (`?ref=<route>`). No cash rewards to children. Irish DPC Fundamentals. |
| One route at a time | Two or three tries a week, each measurable, kept or dropped on evidence. |

## Route types

| Route | How it reaches players | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Founder's network | Players the founder knows try one session and say what was confusing (E0) | Small, but the best feedback; the founder sends. |
| Online communities | Subreddits, Discord servers, forums, Facebook groups for parents and coaches | Self-promotion rules; Reddit's culture punishes ads. |
| Adult creators and trainers | A YouTube or TikTok basketball trainer mentions or uses Deadeye | Must be adults; disclosure; no money talk to kids. |
| Partners | Outdoor courts, camps, 3x3 events, sports shops, youth centres: a QR poster or a challenge | Get permission; no data collection at events. |
| Events | A shooting challenge at a court or camp, leaderboard for the day | Parent consent for photos; no prizes that pressure. |
| Referrals | An in-app invite from a player to a friend | Non-cash rewards only; safety-reviewer first. |
| Play Store | Listing title, short description, screenshots, store experiments | Honest claims only (no "never misses"). |

## Method

1. **Read the numbers.** Arrivals and first sessions last week, by route
   where the counts allow.
2. **Review routes.** Each `live` or `sent` route: what it brought; keep,
   change or drop. A route quiet for three weeks is `paused`.
3. **Find routes.** Public sources only: community directories, search,
   Basketball Ireland and local council event listings, camp pages. Note
   size, activity (posts this month), age mix and rules.
4. **Rank** by likely active players per hour of the founder's time:
   audience fit (young players who play now) x trust (an adult or a
   community they already use) x effort.
5. **Research** the top route before drafting: what gets upvoted or
   shared there, what got removed, who moderates (the role, not the
   person).
6. **Draft** for the founder, in their voice, with a tracking link per
   route. One ask. Under 150 words unless the community likes long posts.
7. **Product gaps.** What would make the route work better (a referral
   invite, a challenge link, a share card): a `G<n>` item.
8. **Next week**, compare and write the lesson.

## Trade tools

- **Gmail drafts** (`create_draft`, `update_draft`, `list_drafts`,
  `get_draft` only): an email to an organisation or adult creator straight
  into the founder's drafts with no recipient filled in; the founder adds
  the address and sends. Never send, read the inbox or search mail.
- **Canva** (`mcp__plugin_marketing_canva__*`): a court poster or event
  flyer with a QR code, from the designer's templates. Drafts only.
- **WebSearch, WebFetch**: finding communities, events and creators, and
  reading community rules.

Each needs the founder's one-time sign-in. If one is not connected, keep
the draft in `docs/agent-os/outreach/` and say so.

## Quality bar

- Each draft could only have been written to that community or partner.
- Each route has a tracking link and a keep-or-drop line.
- No private person's details anywhere; creators by type with a public
  link to their channel only in the founder's private notes, never here.
- Every rule of the community quoted or linked next to the draft.

## Common mistakes

- Posting the same ad to ten communities.
- Counting sign-ups and calling it a win when nobody logged a session.
- Drafting a DM to a teenage player.
- Offering a gift card or cash for referrals to under-18s.
- A creator deal without an "ad" label.
- Claims the app cannot back up.
