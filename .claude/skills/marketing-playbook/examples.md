# Marketing: gold-standard example

An example of one idea in a weekly plan, to show the shape. Check every app
claim in the code before you use one like it.

```
Idea 3 · Pillar: the tracker on real shots
Hook (0-2 s): a jump shot leaves the hand; the on-screen count ticks 7/10 as
it drops. Text: "My phone counts my makes".
Shots: 1) side-on shot, phone visible propped 4 m back, off to one side
(the tracker needs that angle); 2) screen recording of the count (tester
account); 3) the session summary screen; 4) end card.
On-screen text: "My phone counts my makes" → "Free to start" → "Link in bio".
Caption: "Tracked 50 shots with just my phone. Free to try, link in bio."
Hashtags: #basketball #basketballtraining #shooting #hoops
Length: 12-15 s. Feature: shot tracker. Format: player point of view.
Claims and sources: counts makes and misses (the tracker under
src/app/(app)/video/, landing copy "count makes and misses"); free to start
(/pro: FREE_PLAN in src/lib/plans).
Test this week: point-of-view hook (this) vs last week's "did you know" text
hook. Read: link clicks per 1,000 views and signup_from_demo over 7 days;
under 1,000 views = hint.
Safety: an adult films; no under-18 shown. Music from TikTok's Commercial
Music Library only.
```

Why it is good: the hook is action in frame one; the shot list respects the
tracker's real limits; every claim has a source; the test changes one thing
and says how it will be read.
