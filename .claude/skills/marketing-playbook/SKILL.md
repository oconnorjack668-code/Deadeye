# Marketing playbook

A professional social marketer knows who the content is for and what it
should make them do, runs every week as a test with one thing changed, and
reads results in sign-ups and first sessions, not only views. With a young
audience they keep the content honest, kind and inside the rules. The rules
in `.claude/agents/marketing.md` win over this file. Video editing belongs to
the video-editor. A gold-standard plan entry to aim at: `examples.md` in
this folder.

## Standards you work to

| Standard | What it means here |
| --- | --- |
| One audience, one job | Basketball players 13 to 18 in Ireland who want to get better. The job: open the app and log a first session. |
| Content pillars | Three to four repeating themes (for example: the tracker on real shots, drills that work, progress stories from adults or consenting players, founder build-in-public). Every idea maps to one. |
| Hook in the first two seconds | Frame one is action; the promise is clear by second two; the app is on screen by second three. |
| One variable per test | Each week tests one thing (hook style, length, format) against the last, with how it will be read. |
| Platform guidance | TikTok Creative Center and TikTok for Business, Meta for Business Reels guidance: native, vertical, sound on with captions, safe zones. |
| ASAI Code, children section, and platform rules | No pester power, no pressure, no claims the app cannot back up, commercial content labelled, music only from the commercial libraries. |
| Honest claims | Every number and claim is checked in the code or `/pro` and written down with its source. |

## Method

1. **Read the situation.** The funnel's biggest drop, last week's results
   (`docs/agent-os/marketing/results.md`), what was posted.
2. **Research.** What formats are working now for basketball and training
   creators (WebSearch; when Similarweb or Ahrefs are connected, where the
   landing page's visitors come from and what people search for around
   "basketball training app"). Formats and hooks, never people's details.
3. **Plan the week.** Ideas, each with pillar, hook, shot list, on-screen
   text, caption, hashtags, length, the feature shown, and the claim sources.
   One test, with how it will be read (link clicks, sign-ups answering
   TikTok, Instagram or YouTube at sign-up, first sessions; plain links, no
   codes).
4. **Brief the video-editor** with a beat-by-beat script for each approved
   idea (hook, captions, voice lines, end card, checked claims).
5. **Visuals that are not video** (covers, carousels, story frames): draft
   in Canva when connected, from the brand style in `docs/design.md`.
6. **Peer review.** safety-reviewer checks anything showing a person who may
   be under 18, and any upsell copy, before the founder sees it.
7. **Read results.** When the founder pastes numbers, compare with the
   week's test. Under about 1,000 views it is a hint. Write what to keep or
   drop, and add the lesson.

## Trade tools

- **Canva** (`mcp__plugin_marketing_canva__*`): covers, carousels, story
  frames. Draft only; never publish or share outside the founder.
- **Figma** (`mcp__plugin_marketing_figma__*`): read the brand and app
  designs for exact colours and screens.
- **Ahrefs** (`mcp__plugin_marketing_ahrefs__*`): search terms around
  basketball training, for captions, the landing page and SEO.
- **Similarweb** (`mcp__plugin_marketing_similarweb__*`): where competitors'
  and the landing page's traffic comes from.

Each needs the founder's one-time sign-in. If one is not connected, say so
and carry on without it.

## Quality bar

- Every idea has a pillar, a hook a stranger would stop for, and a feature.
- One test per week, with how it will be read.
- Every claim has a source.
- Nothing would embarrass the founder in front of a parent.

## Common mistakes

- Planning for views when the goal is a first session.
- Changing three things at once, so nothing can be learned.
- A hook that starts with the logo.
- Copying a creator's exact idea instead of the format.
- "Never misses", "100% accurate", "go pro", "last chance".
