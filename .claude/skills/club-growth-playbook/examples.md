# Club growth: gold-standard examples

Examples of the standard to aim at. The club is made up; never send these.

## Example 1: outreach draft

```
To: the club's public contact form (founder adds the address)
Subject: Shooting practice between sessions for your U14s and U16s

Hi,

I'm the founder of Deadeye, a free basketball training app built in
Wexford. I noticed your U14 and U16 teams both play in the area league
this season.

A lot of coaches tell us players want to shoot between sessions but
nobody can see what they did. Deadeye lets a player track shots with
their phone, and lets a coach set a weekly drill and see who did it. No
private messages between coaches and players, and under-16s need a
parent's approval to join.

Would one of your coaches like to try it with one team for two weeks?
I can do a 15-minute call on Tuesday 18th at 7pm or Thursday 20th at 6pm.

Thanks,
<founder>
```

Why it is good: about this club, starts from the coach's problem, answers
the safeguarding question before it is asked, a small ask with two real
times (from the calendar), no attachment.

## Example 2: trial plan (summary)

```
Club: <club> U16 boys · Stage: trial from 2026-11-24
Set up: head coach (adult, declares Garda vetting in the app), makes the team,
shares the join link with the parent message for under-16s.
Week 1: each player logs one session; coach posts one assignment.
Week 2 check-in (founder, 10 min call): what got used, what got in the way.
Success line (agreed with the coach before the start): 8 of 12 players log
a first session within 7 days, and the coach posts an assignment both weeks.
End (2026-12-08): success → keep going free with one team, offer Pro for
more teams; not met → ask why, fix one thing or pause.
Safeguarding: Children's Officer told about the trial by the coach; no
private coach-player messages in the app; no player data beyond the coach
screens.
```

Why it is good: the success line and end decision are written before it
starts, the volunteer's work is small, safeguarding is built in.
