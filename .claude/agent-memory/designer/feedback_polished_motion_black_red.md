Every design must have smooth animation and look nice and well made, never simple or unpolished (founder, 2026-11-22). Brand is black and red: red for the logo and slogans ("AIM IS EARNED"), orange for actions. Sleek, professional, easy to understand, cool, never busy.

**Why:** the founder rejected plain restyles on 2026-11-20 as "less cool" and the three full restyles as "too much"; the target sits between.

**How to apply:** deliver motion as a watchable HTML prototype plus an MP4 rendered frame by frame (restart animations, pause in the same tick, set currentTime per frame via CDP; see `docs/agent-os/design/mockups/2026-11-22-loading-capture.mjs`). Animate transform and opacity only. Give a reduced-motion still. Rotating text needs a WCAG 2.2.2 answer (pause, or change per visit). See [[designer knowledge file]] `docs/agent-os/knowledge/designer.md`.
