Agent: Claude Code, Opus 5.5 · Commits: see `git log exp/home-club` · Status: in progress (pull request open; needs 00051)

## Outcome
Settings → Your club: players search 275 clubs by name, other name (Wexford Blazers) or town, or say they are
not at a club, or send "my club isn't listed" (name, town, county). Owner tools → Club suggestions groups
them with a ready directory row. Players can opt in to a club badge on their player card. The Monday counts
file adds players with a club, clubs with 5 or more players, and suggestions waiting.

## Decisions
- The club lives in `player_clubs`, readable only by the player, not on `profiles` (founder asked for
  privacy-first; a profile policy change can never expose it).
- Card badge is opt-in. 16+: anyone who can open the card. Under 16: friends and teammates only, whatever
  the switch says (`player_club_badge`). Founder asked for an opt-in badge; the under-16 rule is Claude's.
- Counts name a club only with 5+ players (`CLUB_COUNT_MIN`); suggestions never show who sent them.
- Not added to onboarding: setup is where most new players already stop.
- Privacy policy paragraph added (legal text): the founder approves it by merging.
