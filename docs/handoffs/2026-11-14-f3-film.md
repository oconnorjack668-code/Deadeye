## Outcome
The Film tab on the IQ hub is built: clips from YouTube that stop at each
question (shown below the video), with explanation, See it again, Continue,
captions and speed; packs; saves; private notes; coaches build packs, set a
clip or pack for a team and see done and score plus the team split; an owner
clip picker that checks embedding with oEmbed. No clips seeded.

## Decisions
- Section 11 answered with the recommended options (founder said "do and build all"): coaches see done/score and totals, never answers; Free = clips and packs marked free; assigned film always opens.
- A clip in a free library pack opens on Free: a free pack means its clips.
- Coach packs: 30 per coach, 20 clips each; a Pro clip needs a coach with Pro to go in a pack or be set (00062 triggers), so team film cannot unlock Pro for a free coach.
- The IFrame API attaches to a nocookie iframe built by `youtubeEmbedUrl` (no undocumented `host` option), loaded only after the tap.
- If the API cannot load, the questions come one after another below the video.
- YouTube policy III.F.3.a (no charging to watch embedded content) conflicts with Pro clips: built as asked, raised in S28 for the founder.
