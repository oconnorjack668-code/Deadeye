# Film clips to pick

For the founder, with the owner tool (Owner tools, Demo videos, Film clips).
Film (F3 in `docs/plans/film-and-plays.md`) ships with **no clips**: a
made-up YouTube id would be a broken video, so every clip is a real video
the founder picks. The Film tab says "Clips are on the way" until the first
one is published.

## How to add one

1. Find a video on an **official channel**: a league, a federation, a club or
   a team (for example Basketball Ireland, FIBA, EuroLeague, a college
   conference). Not fan uploads or re-uploads of TV games.
2. Copy its link from the Share button, open Owner tools, Demo videos, Film
   clips, and paste it.
3. Set the start and end (m:ss). 20 to 60 seconds is the target; 10 minutes
   is the most the database takes.
4. Write 1 to 3 questions. Each one stops the clip at its own moment (m:ss),
   has 2 to 4 answers, one right, and a short "why" shown after answering.
   Stop the clip just before the decision, so the player has to read it.
5. Check the video is **not Made for Kids** (YouTube API policy III.E.4.j)
   and tick the box. One sign: its comments are turned off.
6. Save. The tool asks YouTube whether the video may play inside other
   sites, and refuses it if not. Leave "Published" off until you have
   watched it through on your phone.
7. Free or Pro: about 15 clips free in all (plan section 9). The two library
   packs ("Start here: spacing and help defence" and "Reading the
   pick-and-roll") are free; fill them under Film packs, then publish them.

Rules from the safety review (plan section 8): store only the link, start
and end, never download; no clips showing children who are not ours to
show; words written for a 13-year-old.

## The 8 themes

### Spacing
- **Clip**: a half-court possession where one drive works because the
  corners and wings stay wide, or fails because two players stand close.
- **Questions**:
  - The ball is driven from the wing. Where should the corner player be?
    (stay in the corner / cut to the elbow / come to the ball)
  - Why was the paint open for the drive? (two players stayed wide / the
    defence fell asleep / the big set a screen)
  - Two attackers are 6 feet apart. What should one of them do? (move away
    to the next spot / stay / ask for the ball)

### Help defence
- **Clip**: a drive from the wing where the weak-side defender helps from
  the gap, then recovers to their player.
- **Questions**:
  - Your player is on the far side. Where do you stand? (in the gap, one
    foot in the paint / touching your player / at the three-point line)
  - The ball is driven. Who helps first? (the closest player in the gap /
    the player guarding the ball / nobody)
  - After helping, what next? (recover to your player / stay in the paint /
    chase the ball)

### Closeouts
- **Clip**: a kick-out to a shooter and a defender running out, one under
  control with short steps and a high hand, one flying past.
- **Questions**:
  - What do you do in the last few steps? (short choppy steps, hand up /
    sprint all the way / jump at the shooter)
  - The shooter drives past the closeout. Why? (the defender ran too fast
    to stop / the shooter was quicker / bad luck)
  - Which hand is up? (the hand on the shooter's shooting side / either /
    both down)

### Pick-and-roll reads
- **Clip**: a high ball screen where the big defender steps up to the
  handler and the roller is open, then one where the defender drops and the
  handler pulls up.
- **Questions**:
  - The big defender steps up to the handler. Where is the pass? (to the
    roller / back to the top / to the corner)
  - The big defender stays back. What is open? (the pull-up shot / the lob /
    nothing)
  - Where does the screener go after the screen? (roll to the basket / stay
    / pop to the corner)

### Transition
- **Clip**: a defensive rebound and the first three seconds after: an
  outlet, lanes filled, a 3-on-2.
- **Questions**:
  - You got the rebound. What first? (outlet to the guard / dribble the
    length of the floor / wait for everyone)
  - Which lane do you run in? (an empty wide lane / behind the ball / the
    middle with the ball)
  - It is 3-on-2. Who gets the ball? (whoever the defence leaves / always
    the shooter / the fastest player)

### Out of bounds
- **Clip**: a baseline out-of-bounds set from a box or stack, where a
  screen frees a cutter at the rim or a shooter in the corner.
- **Questions**:
  - Who is the first look? (the cutter off the screen / the safe pass /
    the inbounder)
  - Where does the screener go after screening? (to the basket / out to the
    top / stay)
  - Nothing is open. What now? (pass to the safe player at the top / throw
    it anyway / call time-out every time)

### Press
- **Clip**: a team beating a full-court press by flashing a player to the
  middle and passing ahead, and a team getting trapped in the corner.
- **Questions**:
  - Where is the gap in most presses? (the middle / the corners / the
    sideline)
  - You catch it in the middle. What next? (turn and look up the floor /
    dribble back / hold it)
  - Why did the trap work? (the ball went into the corner / the passer
    looked up / the inbounder was quick)

### Shot selection
- **Clip**: a possession that ends in an early contested shot, and one where
  the extra pass gets an open shot.
- **Questions**:
  - Was that a good shot? (no: a defender was on them and it was early /
    yes / it went in, so yes)
  - Who was open? (the corner player one pass away / nobody / the big)
  - What makes a shot good? (open, in range, in rhythm / any shot that goes
    in / a shot by the best player)
