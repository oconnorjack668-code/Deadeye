Built for young players

  • Head coaches are 18 or over and confirm Garda vetting when they set up.
  • No private messages. Coaches post to the whole team.
  • Coaches see weekly training totals. More only if a player chooses to share, and never their notes or videos.
  • Players under 16 need a parent or guardian's approval.
  • Coaches and players under 18 can't be friends on Deadeye.
  • A team joins its club board only after we check with the club.
  • Shot tracking runs on the phone, so videos stay there unless a player uploads one.
  • Players can download or delete all their data at any time.

Getting started

  1. A coach signs up free and creates the team. It takes about two minutes.
  2. Share the team code, QR code or link. Deadeye writes the message for parents.
  3. Players join free and start logging sessions straight away.
  4. Move to a Team Pass or the Club plan when you're ready. Players keep everything they've logged.

Paying

  • Pay once for the season, or monthly. The season price is the better deal.
  • The season runs 1 September to 31 August, so players keep Pro through the summer.
  • Card or SEPA Direct Debit. Clubs can pay by invoice.
  • Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time.
  • Joining mid-season? Start monthly.
