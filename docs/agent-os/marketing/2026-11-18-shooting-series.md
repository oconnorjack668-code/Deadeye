## The series in one paragraph

Same bones every time, so a stranger knows what they are watching in one second: a day count, a number of shots, a spot, the real result, the app's own screen. Question that runs through it: can logging my shots make me a better shooter? Day 1 and Day 14 repeat the same session, so the answer is a fair comparison, not a feeling. Bad days stay in. Four posts a week, same video on TikTok and Reels.

## Rules for this series (read before filming)

1. **The founder is under 18** (ideas.md, 23 Oct). A face, voice or full body he can be known by is an identifiable under-18. Default: filmed from behind or the side at distance, no face in frame, no school, club kit, crest, court name or signage, captions not voice. A face or voice-over only with a parent's written consent for that piece. Ask for one blanket consent for the series if you want faces. No blur or dark patch as a fix; re-crop or reshoot.
2. **Nobody else on camera.** Film when the court is empty or frame them out. Anyone else needs their own agreement (parent's, if under 18).
3. **App screens are the tester account.** Record the sessions on the tester account, so no real username or name is on screen. Day-count stats then belong to the tester account: fine, it is your series account.
4. **The question wording.** Keep "tracking every shot" as your own habit ("I log every shot I take"), never as what Deadeye does. On screen say "Can I get better by logging my shots?" Never "Deadeye counts every shot", "never misses", "100% accurate", "go pro". The app's calls can be wrong: when you fix one, say so on screen ("Deadeye called 31. I fixed 1."). That honesty is a hook, and the review screen is real (checked in the 9 Nov scripts).
5. **Numbers on screen come from the app's results screen** for that session, or your own count if you logged by hand. Never round up. Before each caption, open the results screen and read what is actually on it; this plan only relies on made, taken and percentage, and the court spots (`src/lib/stats.ts` has the spots: Free Throw, five 3pt spots, Deep 3pt).
6. **Sound.** Export with no music in the file: court sound, swish, the app's ticks. Add a library track inside each app: TikTok Commercial Music Library on TikTok, Meta Sound Collection on Reels, quiet. Never reuse the TikTok track on Instagram. Works fully muted because of captions.
7. **Link.** "Link in bio" only after you confirm the bio link is live on that account (TikTok needs a business account or 1,000 followers; unconfirmed in STATUS). Until then end on the next day's teaser, no web address.
8. **Competition lines** (3 posts: 4, 7, 8). Source: the founder's brief, 18 Nov. The public page is `deadeye.ie/competition` (until the domain is bought this week: `deadeyeapp.vercel.app/competition`); the bio link must open that page, and posts say "link in bio", never a typed address. Winners are judged on the most useful feedback, not drawn: say "judged", never "draw", "raffle" or "lucky". Safety-reviewer checks every competition line. Safe line: "Founding Competition. Free to enter. Closes 31 Dec. Best feedback wins. Details at the link in bio." Prizes and "25% off" are money talk and the audience includes 13 to 15 year olds: show them on the page, not in the post, unless safety-reviewer says otherwise. Never "second prize".
9. **Hashtags.** `#DeadeyeDaily` always, plus 3 to 5 from your rotation list. Skip giant tags. `#DeadEye`: my web search found nothing for it and I cannot see TikTok or Instagram search from here. "Dead Eye" is also a well-known gaming term, so it is probably crowded with unrelated content. Open it in each app's search first; use it only if the top results are basketball. Also search `#DeadeyeDaily` once to check nobody else uses it.

## The format (every post, 15 to 22 seconds)

| Time | What |
|---|---|
| 0 to 3s | The opening (three versions per post below). Frame one is a shot in the air, never a logo or black card. |
| 3 to 14s | Cut-down of the session: 4 to 6 shots in real time, the count ticking, the make sounds. Day card stays top-left in the upper band. |

| 19 to 22s | End card, one line, once the next day is known: "Day N next: [session]". Competition posts add the competition line. "Free to start" appears on at least one card per post (answers "free?"). |

Captions in the app's own style (white Anton on the plate, `video-style.md`). Tracker's MAKE/MISS banners and the streak flame are never the hero (founder, 9 Nov): lead with the real shot, the ball going in, the numbers. Lead with what is ours: Ireland, effort, streaks.

## The test (one thing changed)

**Test: how the first 3 seconds open.** Everything else stays the same.

- **Opening A, action first:** a shot in the air, the count ticking, "DAY N" small.
- **Opening B, number first:** the final score big over the ball in flight ("31/50"), then it plays back.
- (Opening C in each post below is a spare: a question over a shot. Not in the test.)

Order, balanced so no opening gets only the easy sessions: Week 1 posts 1 A, 2 B, 3 A, 4 B. Week 2 posts 5 B, 6 A, 7 B, 8 A. Sessions differ, so this is a hint, not proof. It reads from average watch time and the 3-second hold, not likes. Under about 1,000 views it is a hint.

## How to film this alone in 10 minutes

**You need:** phone with Deadeye signed in to the tester account, a ball, a hoop with room behind the shooting spots, something to prop the phone on (a bag, a bin, a wall). No tripod yet; a new one is coming.

1. **Before you leave (2 min):** charge the phone to 50% or more, free some storage, clean the lens, turn on Do Not Disturb, set the phone to portrait, sound on. Open Hoops, New session, pick the drill preset, and check the tester account is the one signed in.
2. **At the court (2 min):** empty court only; if someone arrives, stop or reframe so they are out of shot. Prop the phone 3 to 6 m back, off to one side, at about hoop height or just below, rim in frame with room above it. Angle matters more than distance. Look at what the camera sees before you start.
3. **Rim (1 min):** tap "Find the hoop for me", check both markers on the rim, drag if off. It can be slow on first press; wait for it.
4. **Shoot (4 min):** start tracking, say nothing, move between spots. Take the 50. If the ball goes out of frame, leave it and keep going; fix calls afterwards.
5. **Save (1 min):** open the review screen, fix wrong calls, count them (you will say "fixed [y]" on screen). Tap "Video with the tracker", Vertical, slow-mo on, save. Screen-record the results screen. Note made, taken, percentage and the day count before you leave.

**Send the video-editor:** the saved tracker video, the results-screen recording, the chosen opening (A, B or C), the numbers, the day count, the next day's session, and whether a competition line is allowed on that post. Ask them to keep the make sounds and cut every 1.5 to 3 seconds with the payoff held longest.

**If it goes wrong:** a bad angle means reshoot the next day and keep the day count; a failed tracker session means log it by hand (Hoops, New session, enter the totals) and film 15 seconds of shots; no good footage means a results-screen-only post is still a post. A wrong live call stays in if it is true; say so on screen.
