## Rules for all three

- **Who is in shot:** the founder or a consenting adult, filmed from behind. The tracker video records
  whoever is in frame, so before posting, scrub it and cut any moment a face shows (walking back for a
  rebound is the usual one). No club kit, school crest, town or court signage. Nobody else in shot
  unless they agreed, with a parent's written OK if they are under 18.
- **App screens:** tester account only.
- **Links:** TikTok has no bio link (founder confirmed 2026-11-09), so TikTok captions and every end
  card give the address, deadeyeapp.vercel.app. "Link in bio" is for Instagram captions only.
- **On-screen text:** ours goes in the band about 15% to 30% down from the top, clear of the app's
  score and inside the box that clears both Meta and TikTok: x 140 to 940, y 270 to 1250 px on
  1080x1920. If it covers the app's score, move it to the middle left, still inside the box. Each line stays up for at least 0.8s and at most 17
  characters a second. Timings below already follow that.
- **Sound:** keep the real swish and the app's count tick. Add music only from TikTok's Commercial
  Music Library (TikTok) or Meta's Sound Collection (Instagram), low, about 15 to 20%. Never use the
  same TikTok library track on Instagram.
- **Labels:** turn on TikTok's commercial content setting. If you add a synthetic voice, turn on the
  AI label on both apps. No voice is needed; the text carries every script with the sound off.
- **Comments:** ask for numbers, not videos. Delete or hide any comment that gives a school, town,
  age or full name. Don't repost or duet players' clips.
- **Before posting:** run the checklist in `knowledge/marketing.md`, watch at real speed on a phone,
  and pause on stills to check the rim line sits on the ring, not the bracket.
