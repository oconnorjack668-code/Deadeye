## Before you post: checklist

- [ ] **Who is in the footage.** The makes and misses and the closing shot show shooters from
      behind. The form check clip (about 11 to 13s) shows a player's face clearly, in team kit, and
      someone stands in the background of the rim-tap shot (about 10s). If any of them is under 18,
      you need a parent's written consent for this video. Without it, re-cut with those frames
      blurred or swapped. Adults: get their OK.
- [ ] **The address.** The live site is `deadeyeapp.vercel.app`, and the end card already shows it,
      so the video and captions match. deadeye.ie is not bought yet; when it is, update the bio
      link and re-render the end card.
- [ ] **The demo plays on your phone.** Open deadeyeapp.vercel.app on mobile data (not wifi), scroll to the
      demo, press play. It should count the clip without "Starting..." sticking (fixed 23 Oct).
- [ ] **Music from the commercial library** (see Music below). Business accounts on both apps.
- [ ] **Bio link set to deadeyeapp.vercel.app** on TikTok and Instagram. Bio text: "Basketball training app.
      Shot tracker, drills, IQ. Free to start. Try the demo, no sign-up."
- [ ] **The founding offer is still open.** The video says the first 100 players get 3 months of Pro.
- [ ] **Decide: AI label.** The voice is synthetic (Kokoro TTS). If TikTok or Instagram asks whether
      the post has AI-generated content, say yes. It costs little and keeps it honest.

**Flag for the builder (not marketing's files):** the landing page's meta description says
Deadeye "counts every make and miss", and its FAQ says "It calls every shot as a make or a miss".
On the sample clip it missed one make, so these lines say more than we can back up. Viewers from
these posts land there. Suggested: "counts your makes and misses" and "It calls each shot it sees
as a make or a miss, and you can fix any call before you save."

## Music

The files have voice and sound effects but no music. Add it in each app, from the business
libraries only: TikTok's **Commercial Music Library** and Instagram's **Meta Sound Collection**
(on a business account Instagram only offers cleared tracks). Never a trending song or a track
from a personal account's library: on a brand account it can be muted or taken down, and those
tracks are not cleared for business use.

Pick an instrumental (no lyrics, so it does not fight the voice): hip-hop or electronic, about 90
to 110 BPM, with a clear beat that sits under the make/miss sound effects. Set it low (about 15 to
20% in the editor) so the voice and the swish stay clear. Use the same track on both platforms if
it exists in both libraries; it helps the post feel like one campaign.
