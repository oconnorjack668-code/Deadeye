## What works now (step 2, light research; findings in `knowledge/marketing.md`)

- Timed and streak challenges with a number to beat, often with a duet invite: HomeCourt's 60-second
  makes challenges ([NBA](https://pr.nba.com/nba-partnership-nex-team-homecourt-app), dated),
  [Dr. Dish](https://blog.drdishbasketball.com/basketball-drills-streak-shooting-challenge-0) 1-minute
  streaks, make-count workouts ([Youth Inc.](https://www.youth.inc/learn/youth-inc-recommends-five-resources-to-help-improve-your-shooting)); practitioner.
  For us: the app counts on screen, then "what's yours?", which asks for a first session.
