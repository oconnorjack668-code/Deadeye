## First promo in detail (founder's screenshots, 2026-11-18)

**TikTok** (24s video, first 7 days of the retention chart):
- Average watch time 6.06s (about a quarter of the video); 3.9% watched to the end; total play time 1h 57m.
- Retention: "Most viewers stopped watching at 0:01" (TikTok's own note). The opening lost most people.
- Traffic: For You 83.4%, profile 10.7%, other 4.0%, search 1.8%, sound 0.1%.
- Search queries that found it: "vercel.app" 47.6%, then "free basketball training app", "basketball trainer app android", "basketball apps free", "basketball that counts your makes" at 4.8% each. Search is under 2% of views (about 20), but half of those were people typing the link from the caption.
- Viewers: 942, 97% new and 97% non-followers. Age 18-24 45%, 25-34 31%, 35-44 8%, 45-54 13%, 55+ 3%. Male 49%, female 44%, other 7%. Ireland 72.5%, Italy 8.6%, UK 7.3%, US 2.4%, others 5.8%. TikTok's age breakdown starts at 18, so it cannot show how many 13-17s watched.

**Instagram**: views 1,987 (followers 17.5%, non-followers 82.5%), viewers 1,545; interactions 53 (followers 25.5%); likes 24, comments 4, saves 11, shares 0; profile activity 9; follows 9. Still missing: average watch time, skip rate and the retention graph (Reel, View insights).

**What it says**
- The hook is the problem, not the length: most TikTok viewers left at 0:01. Fixing the first second is worth more than anything later in the edit.
- People wanted the link: about 10 searched "vercel.app" because the caption link cannot be tapped. Put the link in the bio (TikTok needs a business account or 1,000 followers for a bio link) and say "link in bio".
- Ireland is the audience it reached (72.5% on TikTok), as intended.
- The visible audience is adults: 18-24 is the biggest group TikTok can show. 13-17s are hidden from this data, so it cannot say whether the target age watched.

## What they suggest (2026-11-09)

- Both are just past 1,000 views: a first read. TikTok viewers engaged more per view, and Instagram
  showed it to more people. Each platform counts a "view" its own way, so compare a post with earlier
  posts on the same platform, not across platforms.
- Likes aren't the goal. The 2 Nov counts show 0 sign-ups from the demo and nobody joining in the 7 days
  before. Until we know the post dates and link taps, we can't tell whether the promo brought anyone in.
- Keep: posting on both, TikTok first. To learn: link taps per 1,000 views, and whether new players log a
  first session.
