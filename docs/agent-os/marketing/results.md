## What they suggest (2026-11-09)

- Both are just past 1,000 views: a first read. TikTok viewers engaged more per view, and Instagram
  showed it to more people. Each platform counts a "view" its own way, so compare a post with earlier
  posts on the same platform, not across platforms.
- Likes aren't the goal. The 2 Nov counts show 0 sign-ups from the demo and nobody joining in the 7 days
  before. Until we know the post dates and link taps, we can't tell whether the promo brought anyone in.
- Keep: posting on both, TikTok first. To learn: link taps per 1,000 views, and whether new players log a
  first session.
