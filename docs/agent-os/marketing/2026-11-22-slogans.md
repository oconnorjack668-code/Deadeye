# Deadeye slogans, 22 Nov 2026

27 candidates (plus 11 verified athlete quotes, numbered 28 to 38, in their own group below) for the rotating slogan (opening animation under the logo, page loading, login and home, and lines while the tracker checks a clip). Idea status: `idea`, nothing approved. Fill in the last column.

How to read it: Chars is the length with spaces; every line is 26 characters or fewer, so each fits under a logo on a 360 px phone. Top 12 = my suggested starting rotation. "Best place" is where the line works best; any line can go anywhere.

## Rules checked on every line

- No "every shot", "never misses", "100%", "go pro", no accuracy claim (knowledge file, 2026-10-23).
- Tracker lines say what the app is doing (watching, checking, following). They never say it got the call right. The tracker can still get a make or miss wrong.
- No shaming or loss framing for 13 to 19 year olds. "Off day?" is kind, not guilt.
- No famous athlete's or brand's slogan, no attributed quotes. "1% better every day" is a common phrase from habit-building talk, not one person's line, so it is free to use; it is a mindset line, not a claim about the app.
- Irish/UK spelling and plain words.

### Dropped (could not verify, or not usable)

- "Rest at the end, not in the middle" (Kobe Bryant): no dated interview or book found, only quote sites.
- "Hard work outweighs talent" and other Kobe lines on quote pages: no primary source found. Wikiquote's sourced Kobe items were not short or on theme. No Kobe quote is included. I can look again if you have a particular one.
- "Mamba Mentality": Kobe Bryant's phrase, used as Nike and Kobe branding. Not usable.
- "I've failed over and over and over again in my life. And that is why I succeed." (Michael Jordan): real, but it is the script of the 1997 Nike "Failure" ad, so it is Nike's copy. Not usable. The "9,000 shots" line is the same ad.
- "Just do it" (Nike) and "Impossible is nothing" (Adidas): brand slogans. Not usable. The popular "Impossible is nothing... Ali" text is not Ali's: Quote Investigator credits Adidas copywriters (2004 campaign).
- "You miss 100% of the shots you don't take" (Wayne Gretzky): Gretzky said a version in 1983 and credited his father in 1996, but the exact wording is not his and the idea is older (Barry Popik). It also uses "100%", which we avoid.
- "Talent wins games, but teamwork and intelligence win championships" (Jordan): only found in secondary lists, not in Wikiquote's sourced Jordan section. Dropped.
- "Do not let what you cannot do interfere with what you can do" (Wooden): Wooden used it (They Call Me Coach, 1972, p. 56), but a similar line printed in 1971 suggests he was not the origin. Dropped.
- "Don't beat yourself" (Wooden, Charlie Rose 2000): ambiguous for kids. Dropped.
- "To be consistently good for a long, long time takes a lot of work" (Dirk Nowitzki, SI): the SI page would not open, so the wording is not confirmed. Dropped.
- "When you love something... you work at it" credited to Stephen Curry: that is Steve Kerr. Used under Kerr (#32).
- Curry, LeBron, Nash, Dirk, Russell (other lines), Serena (other lines): no short, sourced, kid-safe line found in the time I had.
