## Video ideas (all faceless or adult; app screens from the tester account)

**1. One promo, three openings** (the test; the body is the existing 24.5s promo). Feature: shot tracker.
- Hook A (0-3s): live count ticking at 0/0, swish, voice "Set your phone down and shoot." Hook B: first make with count 1/1, text "Your phone just counted that." Hook C: text "Do you count your makes in your head?" over a shot going up.
- Shot list: new 0-3s only, cut onto the current body. Text: the hook line, then "Deadeye" said aloud by 3s. 25s.
- Caption: "Aim is earned. Set your phone down, shoot, and Deadeye counts your makes and misses. Watch it track real shots, link in bio." Tags: #basketball #basketballtraining #shootingdrills #irishbasketball (IG adds #hoops).
- Check first: the form-check clip shows a player's face; if under 18, no parent consent means re-cut.

**2. 5-spot challenge** (adult or founder from behind). Feature: by-hand logger.
- Hook: "5 spots. 5 shots. What's your number?" over a first shot from the corner.
- Shots: 5 spots in 5 quick cuts, then the phone: Hoops, New session, totals entered, score "17/25". Text: spot names, the score. Last line: "Log yours. Free account, link in bio." 25-30s.
- Caption: "5 spots, 5 shots each. Log your total in Deadeye and comment your score out of 25. Any level counts. Link in bio." Scores in comments only, never videos of players.

**3. Log it in 20 seconds, no court needed.** Feature: Enter totals. Aimed at the biggest drop.
- Hook: "Trained today? Log it before you forget." Screen recording: open Hoops, New session, tap a spot, enter makes, Finish, Save, celebration screen. Text: "1 spot. 2 taps." (check the tap count on the day, E1 says Step 1 of 3). 15-20s.
- Caption: "No court? No problem. Log what you shot today in about 20 seconds. Free to start, link in bio."

**4. Fix any call.** Feature: tracker review screen. Hook: "Think it got one wrong? Tap it." Screen recording of a real review, flipping one call before saving. Text: "Fix any call before you save." 15-20s. Caption from 23 Oct plan. Needs a real run; show a wrong call if one happens.

**5. Phone setup in 10 seconds.** Feature: tracker setup. Hook: "Where to put your phone so it can count." Hands only: propping on a bag 3 to 6 m back, off to one side, two taps on the rim edges. Text: "3 to 6 m back. Off to one side. Tap both rim edges." 12-15s. Teaches one thing, sendable to a teammate. Caption: "Phone on a bag, off to one side, tap both sides of the rim. That is the whole setup. Link in bio."

**6. Film IQ: one question.** Feature: basketball IQ. Hook (text on screen): "2 seconds. What is the right read?" Screen recording of one real IQ question, answer revealed with the app's explanation. The founder picks the question and checks it is correct. Text: question, answer, "Deadeye has more." 15s. Caption: "Send this to the point guard in your team. Link in bio."

**7. Real session, raw.** Feature: live tracker. Hook: "10 shots. Phone on a bag. Let's see what it calls." Screen recording of a live run, then results. No edits. 20-30s. Caption from 23 Oct plan.
