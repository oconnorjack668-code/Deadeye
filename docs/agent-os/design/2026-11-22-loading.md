# Loading moments: opening, slogan, page loader, tracker decode, and the logo (22 Nov 2026)

**Problem.** Today the app opens on a blank dark page, shows grey blocks between pages and a plain bar while a clip is checked, and never says "Deadeye". Four small moments can make it feel finished and put the brand (black and red, "Aim is earned") in front of every player without adding waiting. Activation step: open the app and start a first session; the decode screen is seen by every player who uploads a clip.

Founder's standard, 22 Nov: smooth animation, well made, never simple and unpolished, never busy. Red is the logo and the slogan; orange stays for actions.

## Decided by the founder, 2026-11-22

1. The opening rotates **A, B, C: a different one each time a player opens the app.** Cold open only, never on page changes or when returning within the session.
2. A **new slogan each visit**, no live rotation on sign in or home.
3. **Minimum 0.8 s on a cold open, tap to skip at any time.** (No opening is held past its own length: A 1.45 s, C 1.4 s, B about 2.1 s.)
4. Tracker tips **rotate: three changes, then hold on the last line**; tapping the card moves to the next.
5. **Logo: improved C is the logo** (founder, 2026-11-22). The builder replaces the app icon and logo files later; I do not. All prototypes below use logo C.
6. Open: the slogan list (marketing's).

## Trend lines (principles, nothing copied)

- 2026-04-09, NBC's NBA graphics (`trends/visual.md`): snappy animation from dribble rhythm. Take: quick timing, a small overshoot, then still.
- 2025-12, Strava Year in Sport (Rive): "fluid, energetic" motion reads as premium. Take: one choreographed moment, not many effects.
- 2025-09 to 2026-06, NBA on Prime: glowing court lines as a brand device. Take: our own viewfinder brackets.
- WCAG 2.3.3 / C39: reduced motion can be a fade. [W3C](https://w3c.github.io/wcag/understanding/animation-from-interactions.html).
- WCAG 2.2.2 (read 22 Nov 2026): auto-updating content needs pause/stop/hide or control of its rate, no five-second exception. [W3C](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/pause-stop-hide.html). Hence decisions 2 and 4.
- web.dev animations guide (read 22 Nov 2026): keep animation to `transform` and `opacity`. [web.dev](https://web.dev/articles/animations-guide).

## 1. Opening animation (A, B, C in rotation)

**A, Lock on (1.45 s).** The logo's four red brackets close in on the ball like a scope on the rim. 0 to 60 ms plain `#09090b` (the manifest background, so it joins the Android splash). 60 to 520 brackets travel 68 px in (`cubic-bezier(.16,.84,.34,1)`), overshoot 4 px, settle (`cubic-bezier(.4,0,.2,1)`). 100 to 500 ball `scale(.8)` to 1 with a fade. 520 to 840 a 5% thump and one ping ring. 560 DEADEYE rises 8 px; 800 "AIM IS EARNED" rises. One soft red glow behind the ball. Exit 1250 to 1450 (fade, ease-in).

**B, Swish (about 2.1 s), reworked after the founder's notes.** It was too fast, the hoop looked like clip art, and the ball crossfaded into the logo. Now: a black glossy backboard (soft sheen, red base edge, a red target square), a red rim with a highlight, a clean seven-column net that fades toward the bottom, all matching logo C. The ball is **the logo ball itself**, so there is no crossfade. It arcs in from above left (100 to 520 ms, slowing a touch as it reaches the rim: 0.62 to 0.38 px/ms), goes through the net slower (520 to 980 ms) while the net stretches, widens around it, swings in a damped ripple and settles, drops out and grows as it comes toward the camera, then lands with a small squash (1280 ms); the crosshair swings upright as it lands. The hoop is small and far (rim 168 px, ball 103 px), which is why the ball may grow. Two finishes:
- **B1, settle and lock:** the board keeps its red target square, the scene drifts up and fades, then the logo's brackets close in on the landed ball like A (1200 to 1620 ms), wordmark 1420, slogan 1620, exit 1950 to 2150.
- **B2, the hoop becomes the mark (recommended):** the backboard's red target square *is* the logo's four brackets. After the swish they leave the board, fly down over the net and scale up to lock on the ball (about 1000 to 1450 ms, a 4.5% overshoot), while the hoop fades up and away. Wordmark 1340, slogan 1540, exit 1850 to 2050. One idea, one object: the board's target becomes the scope. B1 is safer to build; B2 is the better story.

**C, Reticle (1.4 s).** Four hairlines cross at the ball's centre, the ball is revealed, the brackets settle in (scale 1.16 to 1), then wordmark and slogan. No artwork at all.

**Reduced motion (the still):** the finished lockup at once, held 0.7 s, a 200 ms fade out. B shows no net or ball drop.
**Recommendation:** A is the default and the first one a new player ever sees; B (as B2) and C follow in turn.

**How the app picks the next one.** One counter in `localStorage`, key `dd-open-n`: `n = (stored + 1) mod 3`, written straight away; 0 is A, so a brand-new device gets A first. If `localStorage` throws (private mode, blocked), pick at random. A `sessionStorage` flag `dd-opened` means "already shown this visit", so it never repeats within a session. This runs in the tiny inline script in `<head>`, before first paint, and sets `data-open="a|b|c"` on `<html>`; CSS shows the matching scene. No server, no account.

**Never.** Block the app (it mounts underneath, the overlay only covers it); play on page changes, warm starts or tab switches; run longer than its own length (B is the longest, about 2.1 s); use footage, a player or a name. Timing rule (decision 3): the exit starts when the app is ready, but never before 0.8 s so the lock lands, and never after the animation's own end.

## 2. Slogan

Placeholders until marketing's list is picked: "Aim is earned", "1% better every day", "One more rep". The slot is one line high and never changes height. A line fades in 350 ms with a 7 px rise and out 220 ms; never two lines at once. Style is today's login line: `text-sm font-bold uppercase tracking-[0.2em] text-red-400` (12 px `text-xs` as the home label). On open, sign in and home it is **one line, a new one each visit** (decision 2; picked in a `useEffect`, shown after its own delay, so no flicker). The sign-in frame in the prototype still loops three lines at 6 s so the feel can be judged; it is not the recommended build. Reduced motion: the line appears without the rise.

## 3. Page loading

The fast skeleton shows at 0 ms. Its grey pulse becomes one slow light sweep across the whole skeleton (one `translateX` layer, 1.5 s). A small pill appears 92 px above the bottom, only after **400 ms**: `rounded-full border border-zinc-700 bg-zinc-900/95`, 44 px, the logo ball spinning (1.1 s a turn), one slogan in `text-xs font-bold uppercase tracking-[0.2em] text-red-400`. A page that loads in under 400 ms never shows it. The page replaces skeleton and pill at once, no fade. Reduced motion: no sweep, no spin. `role="status"` reads "Loading"; the slogan is `aria-hidden`.

## 4. Tracker decode

In the `tracking` step: the clip card with four red brackets and one scan line (a red band sweeping down, 2.4 s, only while decoding, since the clip is not playing); a `text-5xl font-black tabular-nums` percent beside "Checking your clip" and "Shots found 7" (real state); an 8 px orange progress bar (the one orange) with a sheen; a card with one line at a time ("Checking every frame without playing it, so no shot is missed between frames.", "Aim is earned", "Every shot can be corrected before you save.", "One more rep"); today's "Stop early and review". **Decided:** the line changes at most three times (about 5 s each in the build; 4 s in the prototype), then holds on the last line; tapping the card moves on. A slow, re-checked or warning state shows today's own message, still. Reduced motion: percent and bar still move, scan, sheen and rotation stop.

## Build (for the builder, after the founder approves)

- **Opening:** `src/components/brand/OpeningSplash.tsx` plus `.dd-open*` keyframes in `src/app/globals.css`, once in `src/app/layout.tsx`, rendered as plain HTML so it paints on the first frame. Always dark (`data-theme="dark"`). `fixed inset-0 z-[100]`, hidden on `animationend` of the exit; `pointerdown` or `keydown` adds `skipped`. Brackets, ball, wordmark are separate boxes with static SVG (`LogoMark` from logo C's SVG, below); text starts at 560 ms, after the fonts load.
- **B's net** is the one non-transform animation: a small SVG path morph (SMIL, 220 x 260 px, about 1 s). Do not ship its 75 sampled frames in the HTML (about 130 KB): generate the `values` at run time only when B is picked, from `nodes()` in `-swish.mjs`; the ball's path and the bracket flight are plain CSS keyframes sampled from the same function. The ball path is sampled every 16 ms so it stays smooth at 60 fps. SMIL works in Safari and Chrome; the CSS `d` property does not in Safari.
- **Slogans:** `src/lib/slogans.ts` (`SLOGANS`), one edit when marketing picks.
- **Loader:** extend `src/app/(app)/loading.tsx`; `PageLoader` pill `animation: dd-loader-in 240ms var(--ease-deadeye) 400ms backwards`; spinner `/logo.webp` at 26 px.
- **Decode:** `src/components/video/DecodeProgress.tsx` in the `tracking` block of `ShotTracker.tsx` (about line 1801). Fill `transform: scaleX(progress)` with `transition: transform 200ms linear`, never `width`; screen-reader update every 25%.
- **Cheap Android:** at most four things move at once on decode, all transform or opacity (B's net aside), no blur or shadow animated. Checked only in headless Chrome frames; before shipping use DevTools 6x CPU throttle and one real low-end phone.
