# Loading moments: opening, slogan, page loader, tracker decode (22 Nov 2026)

**Problem.** Today the app opens on a blank dark page, shows grey blocks between pages and a plain bar while a clip is checked. It never says "Deadeye". Four small moments can make it feel finished and put the brand (black and red, "Aim is earned") in front of every player, without adding a second of waiting. Activation step: open the app and start a first session (the opening and page loader are seen by everyone; the decode screen is seen by every player who uploads a clip).

Founder's standard, 22 Nov: smooth animation, designs that look well made, never simple and unpolished, never busy. Red is the logo and the slogan; orange stays for actions.

Everything is a working prototype in `mockups/` (open in a browser at 390 px, **Replay** and **Reduced motion** buttons at the bottom; add `?cap` to hide them). Each has an MP4 captured frame by frame (so it is exact, not a screen recording) and a PNG still. The ball is redrawn from `public/icon-512.png`; the builder uses the real asset (see Build).

## Trend lines this draws on (principles, nothing copied)

- 2026-04-09, NBC's NBA graphics (`trends/visual.md`): snappy animation taken from dribble rhythm. Take: quick, rhythmic timing, a small overshoot, then still. The brackets overshoot 4 px and settle.
- 2025-12, Strava Year in Sport (Rive): "fluid, energetic" motion makes a moment feel premium. Take: one choreographed moment, not many effects.
- 2025-09 to 2026-06, NBA on Prime: glowing court lines as a brand device. Take: our own viewfinder brackets as the device, the logo already has them.
- WCAG 2.3.3 / C39 (strong): reduced motion does not have to mean none; fades are fine. [W3C](https://w3c.github.io/wcag/understanding/animation-from-interactions.html).
- WCAG 2.2.2 (read 22 Nov 2026): auto-updating content that starts by itself needs a way to pause, stop or hide it, or control how often it updates, with no five-second exception. [W3C](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/pause-stop-hide.html). This shapes moment 2.
- web.dev animations guide (read 22 Nov 2026): keep animation to `transform` and `opacity` so it stays on the compositor; use `will-change` only when needed. [web.dev](https://web.dev/articles/animations-guide). This is the rule for every moment below.

## 1. Opening animation: recommend A, Lock on

| | Idea | Why / why not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A Lock on** | The four red brackets (the logo's own) close in on the ball like a scope locking onto the rim, a single ping ring, then DEADEYE and "AIM IS EARNED" rise in. | **Recommended.** It is the logo's own geometry, no new artwork, the shortest path, and "aim is earned" is literally shown. |
| B Swish | A ball drops through a net, brackets close on it. | Fun and clearly basketball, but the net is new artwork to draw and keep, the ball looks as big as the hoop, and it is the busiest of the three. |
| C Reticle | Four hairlines cross at the centre, the ball is revealed, brackets settle. | Calmest and cheapest. Less "wow"; works as the fallback if A feels too much. |

**A, timeline (1.45 s).** 0 to 60 ms plain `#09090b` (the manifest's `background_color`, so it joins the Android splash with no flash). 60 to 520 brackets travel in 68 px from each corner (`cubic-bezier(.16,.84,.34,1)`), overshoot 4 px at 72% and settle (`cubic-bezier(.4,0,.2,1)`), fading in over 140 ms. 100 to 500 ball `scale(.8)` to 1 plus fade, `var(--ease-deadeye)`. 520 to 840 a 5% thump and a ping ring (`scale 1 to 1.55`, opacity .55 to 0, 560 ms). 560 to 920 DEADEYE rises 8 px and fades in. 800 to 1160 "AIM IS EARNED" the same. A red glow (`rgba(255,36,54,.2)`, one soft radial) fades in behind the ball: the one glow, on the one key element. Exit: 1250 to 1450 the whole screen fades out (ease-in), the app is already there underneath. Tap anywhere: a 150 ms fade, straight to the app.

**Reduced motion (the still).** Everything is drawn in its final state at once, held 0.7 s, then a 200 ms fade out. No travel, scale or ping.

**Never.** Block the app (it mounts underneath; the overlay only covers it); play on page changes, on warm starts or on every tab switch (once per visit, a cold start only); delay the app: the exit starts at the earlier of 1.25 s or the app being ready, but never before 0.8 s so the lock lands (see decision 1); use footage, a player or a name.

## 2. Rotating slogan

Placeholders until marketing's list is picked: "Aim is earned", "1% better every day", "One more rep". The slot is one line high and never changes height, so nothing below it jumps. A line fades in over 350 ms with a 7 px rise, holds, fades out over 220 ms with a 5 px lift, and the next line fades in after it: never two lines at once. Style is today's login line: `text-sm font-bold uppercase tracking-[0.2em] text-red-400`, 12 px (`text-xs`) where it is a small label (home).

- **On open:** one line under the wordmark, picked per open, fades in once, never rotates (the opening is only 1.45 s).
- **Sign in:** recommend **a new line each visit, no live rotation.** WCAG 2.2.2 treats a line that swaps by itself as auto-updating content and asks for a pause control; a pause button on a login card is clutter. The prototype still shows a 6 s rotation so the founder can judge the feel. If the founder wants it live, add a quiet "Pause" and stop on field focus.
- **Home:** one line under "Hey Sam", the same all day, a new one tomorrow. It must not move while a player reads their numbers.
- **Reduced motion:** the first line, no fades.

## 3. Page loading

Keep the fast skeleton, shown at 0 ms (it is today's, so a tap still feels instant). Two changes. The grey `animate-pulse` becomes one slow light sweep across the whole skeleton (a single `translateX` layer, 1.5 s, `cubic-bezier(.45,0,.55,1)`). And a small pill appears at the bottom, 92 px above the safe area, only after **400 ms**: `rounded-full border border-zinc-700 bg-zinc-900/95`, 44 px high, the logo ball spinning (1.1 s per turn) and one slogan in `text-xs font-bold uppercase tracking-[0.2em] text-red-400`. A page that loads in under 400 ms never shows the pill, so the loader cannot make anything feel slower. When the page arrives it replaces the skeleton and the pill at once, no fade (no entrance animations on content: `docs/design.md`). Reduced motion: the sweep and the spin stop, the pill appears without moving.

**Never.** Show the pill under 400 ms; put it over content a player can tap; announce the slogan to a screen reader (the pill is `role="status"` reading "Loading"; the slogan is `aria-hidden`).

## 4. Shot tracker decode

Replaces the plain 6 px bar in the `tracking` step while a clip is decoded. Top to bottom: the clip card with the four red brackets and one scan line (a soft red band sweeping down, 2.4 s, only while decoding: the clip is not playing, so the sweep is honest); a heavy **`text-5xl font-black tabular-nums`** percent with "Checking your clip" and "Shots found 7" (both are real tracker state); an 8 px progress bar (orange fill, the one orange, `from-orange-600 to-orange-400`) with a faint sheen sliding across so a slow start still shows life; a card with one line at a time (the existing "Checking every frame without playing it, so no shot is missed between frames." as the first line, then "Aim is earned", "Good to know: Every shot can be corrected before you save.", "One more rep"), 4 s each in the demo; and today's "Stop early and review" button, unchanged. Reduced motion: the percent and bar still move (they are information), scan, sheen and rotation stop, the first line stays.

- If a clip is slow, re-checked or has a warning, that card shows today's own message, still, because it carries real information. The rotation is only for the plain case.
- WCAG 2.2.2: the line advances by itself, so build it as at most three changes (about 15 s), then it holds, and tapping the card advances it. Founder decision 3.
- The hint, the tips and "Shots found" are placeholders in the prototype; "Good to know" must stay a true statement (it is today's own copy).

## Build (for the builder, after the founder approves)

- **Opening:** `src/components/brand/OpeningSplash.tsx` plus `.dd-open*` keyframes in `src/app/globals.css`, mounted once in `src/app/layout.tsx`. Render it as plain HTML in the server output so it paints on the first frame (the app hydrates underneath). A tiny inline script in `<head>` sets `sessionStorage['dd-opened']` and adds `html.dd-seen`, which hides the overlay, so it plays once per visit; a killed PWA is a new visit. Wrap it in `data-theme="dark"`: it is always dark (the splash is). Overlay `fixed inset-0 z-[100]`, hidden on `animationend` of the exit; `pointerdown` or `keydown` adds a `skipped` class. Brackets are four `div`s with two borders; the ball is its own `div` with a static picture. Every moving thing is its own box (transform and opacity only). Text starts at 560 ms, after the fonts are in, so there is no flash of a fallback font on the first paint. Reduced motion: `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` with the still rules (see the prototype's CSS, it is written this way).
- **Ball artwork:** export the logo once as SVG from the founder's design file (or use `public/icon-512.png`). `public/logo.png` is 256 px: soft at 148 px on a 3x phone. The SVG in the prototype (`2026-11-22-loading-lib.mjs`, `ball()`) can be lifted as `LogoMark.tsx` meanwhile.
- **Slogans:** one file `src/lib/slogans.ts` (`export const SLOGANS`), so marketing's picked list is one edit. Pick with `sessionStorage` per visit in a `useEffect`: the pill and the login line only show after their own delay, so there is no flicker.
- **Page loader:** extend `src/app/(app)/loading.tsx`; the pill is a small `PageLoader` client component with `animation: dd-loader-in 240ms var(--ease-deadeye) 400ms backwards`. The spinner is `/logo.webp` at 26 px, `animation: dd-spin 1.1s cubic-bezier(.5,.1,.5,.9) infinite`.
- **Decode:** `src/components/video/DecodeProgress.tsx` used in the `tracking` block of `ShotTracker.tsx` (about line 1801). Fill `transform: scaleX(progress)` with `transition: transform 200ms linear`, never `width`. A screen-reader line updates at 25% steps in `aria-live="polite"`, not every percent.
- **Cheap Android:** at most four things move at once on the decode screen, all transform or opacity, no blur or shadow animated, no `will-change` outside the overlay while it is mounted. The prototypes use no JavaScript in their loops. This has been checked only in headless Chrome; before shipping, check the builder's version in Chrome DevTools with 6x CPU throttle, and on one real low-end phone.
