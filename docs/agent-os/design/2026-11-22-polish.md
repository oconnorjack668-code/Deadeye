## Shot chart redesign (founder, 2026-11-22; softened the same day)

The founder liked everything else but not the chart: a plain court, soft orange blobs, outlined numbers. Then asked for softer zone edges and smaller labels. Redrawn for the three places it shows. Files (prefix `2026-11-22-shotchart-`): `screen` (the app's shot chart card, today beside new), `result` (the first-session hero: today, new at 390 and at 360 px), `share` (the session card, before and new; full size `2026-11-22-polish-share-session-1920.png`, feed `-1350.png`). The same chart is in `polish-result`, the share cards and `share-overview`. Code: `shotchart-lib.mjs` (zones and geometry from `src/lib/court.ts`).

- **Court.** A dark hardwood half court (warm near-black, faint planks, a soft light over the hoop), crisp white lines: paint with lane marks, free-throw circle (dashed inside the paint), three-point line, restricted arc, backboard, orange rim, half-court arc. Hoop at the top, as in the app. The plank texture is under 8% and sits under the lines.
- **Zones: soft, not blocky.** The 13 real shooting areas (same rules as `classifyZone`) are filled on the scale, feathered with a 7-unit blur (about 4 px) so colours melt into the wood and each other, at 78% opacity so the hardwood shows through. Thin dark-and-light lines mark the borders. A zone with no shots stays bare wood. Still clearly zones: the court lines and borders stay sharp.
- **Labels: one small chip per zone.** Percent in Geist Black 14 px (16 px on the result, 38 px on the share card), makes/attempts beside it at 12 px (the 12 px floor; 11 px would break `docs/design.md`), on a dark chip edged in the zone colour. About 45% less area than the first version, so most of each zone shows. The corner three is only a 3 ft strip, so its chip shows the percent only, against the sideline and inside the court (the 58% no longer sits outside). On a single zone (result, card) the chip sits just below the zone with a pointer so the lit zone stays whole.
- **Colour scale: classic (founder).** Cold `blue-600` under 25%, cool `sky-500` 25 to 33%, average `yellow-500` 33 to 40%, warm `orange-500` 40 to 50%, hot `red-600` 50% and up, the same absolute bands as today. Red here means hot, a good thing, as on most shot charts. A 12 px key with the words, and a list under the court with the exact numbers and the band swatch. Never colour alone.
- **Checks.** 0 contrast fails (`2026-11-22-polish-contrast.mjs`): white and `zinc-300` on the near-black chip. Result hero checked at 390 and 360 px. Not tested: eight zones all lit on a 360 px chart (chips will touch). Builder rule: when two chips overlap, the zone with fewer shots drops to the percent only. Reduced motion: the chart is static.
- **Build.** `ShotHeatMap.tsx`: replace the blobs with zone paths (feathered with an SVG `feGaussianBlur`), chips and the five fills; `CourtMarkings.tsx` stays for the logger. The result and the share card use the same function with a crop (`xRange`, `yMin`) and the chip below the zone.
