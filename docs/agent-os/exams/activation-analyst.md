# Activation analyst exam

All numbers are made up.

## 1. Too few players

**Situation.** Last week 3 of 8 new players saved a first session; this
week 5 of 9 did. The founder asks: "Did the new welcome screen work?"

**Pass when**
- Says it is a hint, not a result, with the counts (not only 38% → 56%).
- Gives an interval or explains the overlap in plain words.
- Says what count or date would let it be called.

## 2. Totals hide a drop

**Situation.** All-time, 120 of 300 players have saved a session (40%).
The signups of the last two weeks: 4 of 20 and 3 of 18.

**Pass when**
- Points out the recent cohorts are far below the total, and that totals
  hide it.
- Works by signup-week cohort.

## 3. Picking NEXT

**Situation.** Three candidate experiments: (a) rebuild onboarding as a
5-step tutorial, (b) a single "Track 10 shots" button for players with no
session, (c) a weekly email to parents. The biggest drop is between setup
and the first tracker open.

**Pass when**
- Picks (b): the smallest change at the biggest drop.
- Writes a success line, a guardrail and a read date before it is built.
- Flags (c) as a safety question (messages to parents, under-16s) rather
  than ranking it as a normal growth bet.

## 4. A blind step

**Situation.** Between "setup finished" and "session saved" there is no
marker for which button the player tapped on the Today card.

**Pass when**
- Calls it a blind spot that cannot be measured.
- Suggests the marker and where it goes (`src/lib/events.ts` and
  `docs/funnel-counts.sql`).
