## 1. E0: the founder's own network (send first)

To about 5 players, or parents of players, the founder already knows, one at a time by text or WhatsApp. Fill the
blank with the founder's own best in the 60-second timed drill, or delete that line.

```
Hey, I've been building a basketball app called Deadeye and I'd like a few people I know to try it before anyone else does.

You prop your phone up a few metres back, off to one side of the hoop, and it counts your makes and misses while you shoot. You can also type your shots in by hand if you'd rather.

Could you do one session this week, even ten minutes, and tell me what was confusing or annoying? Honest beats nice, and a screenshot of anything weird helps.

If you want a target, my best in the 60-second timed drill is __. Beat it.

It's free: https://deadeyeapp.vercel.app
If you're under 16, a parent approves the account with a link you send them, so it takes an extra minute.

Thanks, this really helps me.
```

To a parent: start "Would <first name> try one session this week?" and end "If they're under 16, you'll get a link to
approve the account." Answers (paraphrased, no names) go to the activation-analyst; the 9 Nov report is waiting on them.

## 2. Wexford local media: South East Radio, then the Wexford People (answer: radio or a newspaper)

Why first of the two: one email reaches Wexford households, parents of young players included, for about 20 minutes
of the founder's time, and South East Radio already covers local basketball ([court funding, Oct 2024](https://www.southeastradio.ie/news/wexford/basketball-to-benefit-from-funding-announced-today/)).
Where: [South East Radio contact page](https://www.southeastradio.ie/contact-us/) (general email and phone, Custom
House Quay, Wexford); the Wexford People ([wexfordpeople.ie](https://www.wexfordpeople.ie/), Mediahuis; newsroom
contact printed in the paper). Rule: the editor decides and nothing is paid; a "sponsored" slot would be an ad, labelled,
and the founder's call. The founder is under 18, so a parent agrees before any interview, photo or name in print.
Tracking: ask for the link in the online story. Players who heard it pick "Radio or a newspaper" at sign-up; also
watch for sign-ups that skipped the question in the days after.

```
Subject: Wexford student built a free basketball training app

Hi,

I'm <age>, from <town>, and I've built a free basketball training app called Deadeye. You prop a phone up beside the court and it counts your makes and misses while you shoot. It also has 168 drills and a way to log sessions by hand.

I'm looking for Wexford players to try it and tell me what's confusing, and I thought your listeners might be interested. I'd be glad to come in for a chat, or meet at a court where one of your team could try the 60-second shooting drill and see if they can beat my number.

The app is here: https://deadeyeapp.vercel.app

I'm under 18, so my parent is copied in.

Thanks,
<founder's name>
```

Wexford People version: "readers" for "listeners", and ask that the online story uses the link.

## 3. FDYS, Wexford's youth service (answer: a youth club or youth service)

Why second: youth workers are adults young people already trust, with projects in Gorey, Enniscorthy, Bunclody and
elsewhere in the county; fewer people than radio and more back-and-forth (does any project have a hoop?). Where:
[fdys.ie](https://fdys.ie/) (phone on the home page) or its public Facebook page, FDYS County Wexford. Rule: email the
organisation, never a young person; the service's safeguarding rules apply; nothing collected at a youth night.
Safety-reviewer reads it before it is sent; the designer makes the poster (QR to the plain address `https://deadeyeapp.vercel.app`).

```
Subject: A free shooting game for a youth night

Hi,

I'm a basketball player from <town> and I've built a free training app called Deadeye. Do any FDYS projects have a basketball hoop? If so, a youth night might like this shooting game. It needs no app or phones:

Two players play rock paper scissors before each shot. The loser takes their next shot one step further back. First to three makes wins.

If any young people want to keep practising at home, I could send a poster with the app's link. Players under 16 need a parent to approve their account, and any video stays on the phone. Nobody signs up, gets photographed or gives details at the night itself.

I'm under 18, so my parent is copied in. Happy to answer any questions.

Thanks,
<founder's name>
```
