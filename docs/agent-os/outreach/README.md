# Player outreach

The player-outreach agent's work (`.claude/agents/player-outreach.md`,
`/agent-os outreach`): routes to individual players other than clubs
(club-growth) and Deadeye's own posts (marketing).

- `routes.md`: every route, its stage, tracking link and what it brought.
- `YYYY-MM-DD-outreach.md`: the week's plan and drafts for the founder.

Communities, organisations and creator types only: never a private
person's name, handle, phone or email, and never a player's details. The
founder sends and posts everything. Product ideas go to `../backlog.md`
as `G<n>` items.
