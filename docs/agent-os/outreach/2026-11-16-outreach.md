## 1. E0: the founder's network (answer: A friend or teammate)

Why first: the founder knows these people, so they trust it, and their answers explain the setup-to-first-session drop.
Rule: only people the founder already knows, one at a time, by text or WhatsApp. Not to anyone the founder doesn't know.

First message (use if not sent yet):
```
Hey, I've been building a basketball app called Deadeye and I'd like a few people I know to try it before anyone else.

You prop your phone up a few metres back, off to one side of the hoop, and it counts your makes and misses while you shoot. Or you can type your shots in by hand after.

Could you do one session this week, even ten minutes, and tell me what was confusing or annoying? Honest beats nice. A screenshot of anything weird helps (crop out your email).

If you want a target: my best is __ makes in one minute. Beat it.

It's free: https://deadeyeapp.vercel.app
If you're under 16, a parent approves the account with a link you send them, so it takes an extra minute.
```

Follow-up, 3 or 4 days later, only to people who said they'd signed up:
```
Did you get to a hoop yet? No stress if not. If you trained already, you can type your shots in by hand after, takes 30 seconds. And if something stopped you, tell me what. That's the bit I most need to know.
```
To a parent: "Would <first name> try one session this week?" and "If they're under 16, you'll get a link to approve the
account." Answers (paraphrased, no names) go to the activation-analyst.

<founder's name>
