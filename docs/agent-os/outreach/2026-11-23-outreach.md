## The one route this week: E0 with the competition (answer: "A friend or teammate")

Why this one: it is the smallest step (one message to people the founder already knows, no email, no approval chain, no waiting on an organisation) and the only route fully ready. `/competition` is open, `deadeye.ie` is live (S53), and the entry needs a first logged session, which is the exact step where 24 players have stopped. A friend who tries it and says what stopped them is worth more than a post nobody sees. It also fits one route per answer: marketing's shooting series (S51) brings "TikTok" and "Instagram", so friends stay on "A friend or teammate" and the two can be told apart.

Rules (no community involved, so no self-promotion rule to quote): only people the founder already knows, one at a time, by text or WhatsApp; never a group, never someone they do not know. Ireland only, ages 13 and over ([rules](../../sales/founding-competition-rules.md), section 2). Under 18 needs a parent or guardian to approve by email. No "tell your friends", no countdown. Reviewed against the 18 Nov checklist line: free entry, the parent rule, the close date and the rules link are all in the message. The page's own wording ("Sign up free to enter", one logged session, Enter, one thing liked, one thing to change) was checked in `src/app/competition/page.tsx` today.

**Smallest step: send this to up to 5 friends or teammates (13 or over, living in the Republic of Ireland), today or tomorrow.**

```
Hey, I'm running a competition for Deadeye, the basketball app I built. It's free to enter.

Go to https://deadeye.ie/competition, make a free account and log one training session. If you can't get to a hoop, you can type in a session you already did, it takes about 30 seconds. Then press Enter and write one thing you liked and one thing you'd change. Honest is best, bad feedback counts as much as good.

Winners are judged by two adults on the most useful feedback. The prizes are on that page.

If you're under 16, a parent or guardian approves your account first. If you're under 18, they also get an email to OK your entry, and it only counts once they do. Closes 31 December. Full rules are on that page.

When it asks where you heard about Deadeye, pick "A friend or teammate". And if anything confuses you or stops you, tell me. That's the most useful bit.
```

Follow-up, 3 or 4 days later, only to people who said they signed up (once, then stop):
```
Did you get to a hoop yet? No stress if not. You can type a session in by hand, and that's enough to enter. If something stopped you, tell me what.
```
To a parent: "Would <first name> like to try a free basketball app and enter a competition? If they're under 16 you'll get a link to approve their account. If they're under 18 you'll also get an email to approve their entry." Anyone who says no or goes quiet after the follow-up gets nothing more. Answers go to the activation-analyst paraphrased: no names, no screenshots, nothing that could pick one person out (the repo is public).
