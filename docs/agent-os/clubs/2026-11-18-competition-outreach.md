# Founding Competition: club outreach pack, 2026-11-18 (updated for the judged competition)

Drafts for the founder. Nothing here has been sent. Rules: `docs/sales/founding-competition-rules.md`. Do not send any of this until the rules are published and the checks in their founder box are done (a solicitor should still confirm the judged-competition route, prize value and billing).

## Ground rules for every message

- Send to a club's public channel or a role (secretary, chair, underage coordinator, Children's Officer). Never to a child, and never to a player's own account or number. Players hear about it through the club's adults or the club's own page, and the club decides whether to share it.
- Say "everyone who enters gets 25% off". Never "second prize", "runner-up" or "you have won" to non-winners.
- Winners are judged on the most useful feedback. Never say "draw", "random" or "luck".
- Do not ask for a review anywhere. Feedback is private and can be critical.
- Quote only what is built: Coach Free (one team, codes, assignments, who trained), Team Pass EUR 179 a year or EUR 19.99 a month (up to 15 players), Club plan EUR 899 a year for up to 8 teams. Club billing is not live yet; the offer is open until 31 March 2027, so do not promise a billing date.
- Make the first line about that club. Fill `[ONE LINE]` from the club's own public page (for example "your U12s train Tuesdays", from the club site). If you cannot, do not send.
- At most two messages to a club, about a week apart, then stop.
- Founder sends. Replace `[CLUB]`, `[TOWN]` and `[YOUR NAME]`.
- Copy your parent (the promoter) into every email. Call only a club's public number. If an adult replies privately or asks to meet, your parent answers.

## (a) Cold email to a secretary or chair

Subject: A free tool for [CLUB], and a competition

> Hello,
>
> [ONE LINE about the club from its public page.]
>
> I am 16 and I built Deadeye myself, a basketball training app for Irish players and coaches. I am running a competition: one club wins a year of the Club plan free (up to 8 teams, worth EUR 899), and ten players win Deadeye Pro for life. Winners are judged on the most useful feedback. To enter, a coach (18 or over) sets up one team on the free coach side, one player logs a session, and the coach presses "Enter my club" at deadeye.ie/competition and writes the club's feedback. Everyone who enters gets 25% off.
>
> Head coaches confirm Garda vetting, nothing is private-messaged, and players under 18 need a parent's approval to enter (under-16s also for their account). Could I send your underage coordinator and Children's Officer the rules and a one-page summary? More at deadeye.ie/clubs.
>
> Thanks, [YOUR NAME]

About 150 words. To shorten, cut the safeguarding paragraph to one sentence; keep the line about vetting.

Note: it says "I am 16" because the founder asked for it to show. Remove it if you would rather not name your age in writing.

## (b) Short phone or WhatsApp script

For an adult club official (chair, secretary, underage coordinator), by phone or the club's own public number or page. Not a coach's private number unless the club gave it to you.

> Hi, I am [YOUR NAME]. I built a basketball app called Deadeye and I am running a competition. One club wins a year of the Club plan free and ten players win Pro for life, judged on the most useful feedback. It costs nothing. A coach sets up a team, one player logs a session, and the coach enters the club at deadeye.ie/competition and writes some honest feedback. Everyone who enters gets 25% off after. Would your club give it a go? I can send you the rules.

If they say yes: "The coach needs to be 18 or over, and I can send the Children's Officer the safeguarding details first." If they ask about children's data: "Coaches only see who trained and weekly totals, nothing more. I will send it in writing." Do not promise exports or a club-wide view.

Change from the uncle's line: it says "the coach enters the club" and not "I'll enter you", because the coach has to do it on the website so the club has agreed to it.

## Safeguarding check

Adult-only contact; no player or child data recorded; no private person's details; nothing promised that is not built; no claim that Basketball Ireland approves Deadeye. Marketing should read tone and the safety reviewer should read sections 9 and 11 of the rules before the founder sees them.
