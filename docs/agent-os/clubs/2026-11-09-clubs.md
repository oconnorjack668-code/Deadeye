## Trial plan: Wexford Blazers (when the date is agreed)

- **Who:** one team, head coach as champion. A committee contact (chair or underage coordinator) knows it is running. The club's DSCO (Designated Safeguarding Children Officer) gets the facts below in writing before any player joins.
- **Pick the team with care:** an U12 to U14 team avoids the 16 and 17 gap (parents of 16 and 17 year olds cannot see coach posts yet; see C4). If the club wants an U16 to U18 team, tell the DSCO about that gap first.
- **Day 0:** head coach sets up in the app (club, age 18 or over, Garda vetting declared), makes the team, shares the code, QR code or join link. Under-16s need a parent's approval; hand out the app's parent message where parents are present (a training night), not only by group chat. Founder offers to sit with the coach for setup.
- **Week 1:** most of the team logs a first session; coach posts one assignment.
- **Week 2:** second assignment. Founder checks the Monday counts and asks the coach what is stuck. Coach sets the team's league (the picker lists Wexford; the Kilkenny and South East area board row is `to check`).
- **Week 4:** decision with the committee contact: continue, add teams, or stop. Written down.
- **It worked if:** most of the team (say 8 of 12) logged a first session within 7 days; the coach posted an assignment in weeks 1 and 2; players are still training between sessions in weeks 3 and 4. Readable from "Players on a team", "Assignments posted" and the funnel.
- **If not:** a clean stop. The team is free for one team and nothing is owed.
- **Price if it works:** one team is free. Team Pass is EUR 149.99 per team per season (or EUR 19.99 a month), up to 15 players on Pro. Club plan from 4 teams is EUR 99.99 per team per season. Founding offer, for the first 3 clubs with 4+ teams that use it weekly and share feedback: free to 31 Jan 2027, then EUR 59.99 per team per season; closes 31 Dec 2026. Quote it whole or not at all. Whether the Blazers qualify is the founder's call.
- **Safeguarding facts for the DSCO:** head coaches are 18+ and confirm Garda vetting in the app (this is not a Basketball Ireland licence and does not replace one). No private messages: coaches post to the whole team. Under-16s need a parent's approval and those parents see every coach post. Coaches see weekly totals and who trained, not more. Tracking runs on the phone and videos stay there unless a player uploads one. Do not say or hint that Basketball Ireland approves Deadeye.
- **Not promised:** exports, a club-wide view of children's data, sharing with a school. Any such request goes to the founder as a data-protection question.
- **Club board:** the founder approves each team's board after checking with the club; offer it only in week 2 or later, once there is more than one team.
