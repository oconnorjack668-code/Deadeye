# Visual trends: what looks good now

Kept by the designer; read by marketing, the video-editor and the builder.
See `README.md`. Sources and strength in `../knowledge/findings/designer.md`.

Last refreshed: 2026-11-11 (seeded from the designer's first research)

- 2026-11 · Nike's 2025 youth campaign ("Why Do It?") went grainy, high-contrast, handheld,
  jump cuts, animated type, and showed the miss before the make. Take: raw phone footage,
  hard contrast, moving type; show the miss, then the make ("Aim is earned").
- 2026-11 · NBA 2025-26 City Edition: black bases, one strong accent, local stories. Take:
  black plus one accent is current (that is us: zinc plus orange); local identity comes from
  Irish places (club, county, court), never NBA city looks.
- 2026-11 · Teens meet basketball through clips and individual players, not leagues. Take:
  design for the player's own made shot, number and name; every result screen should survive
  a screen recording.
- 2026-11 · Sports media house style: heavy sans headline over a real photo, built from
  templates. Take: heavy Geist headline over the player's own camera frame, never a cut-out pro.
- 2026-11 · Overtime: named things players can belong to, with attitude. Take: name levels,
  drills and challenges with personality, not "Level 3".
- 2026-11 · 2K's player card (ratings, badges, progress). Take: a personal "player card"
  idea, not 2K's art or layout.
- 2026-11 · Strava Year in Sport: personal recap, lightly animated, the most shared fitness
  format. Take: a free season or month recap card for every player.
- 2026-11 · WHOOP home: at most three dials, tap for the breakdown. Take: three headline
  stats on Today (makes, streak, accuracy), detail one level down.
- 2026-11 · Canva 2026 "Imperfect by Design": DIY, collage, tape, grain, scribbles. Take: on
  social and share cards only; numbers stay crisp; the app UI stays clean.
- 2026-11 · Rough layer plus clean type is the sports-media formula. Take: one textured layer
  at most, never everything rough.
- 2026-11 · Avoid: emerald and shamrock (reads as Basketball Ireland), red-versus-green only
  for MAKE/MISS, a dying flame for a missed day.
