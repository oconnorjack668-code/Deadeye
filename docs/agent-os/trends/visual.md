# Visual trends: what looks good now

Kept by the designer; read by marketing, the video-editor and the builder.
See `README.md`. Sources and strength in `../knowledge/findings/designer.md`.

Last refreshed: 2026-11-20 (visual-direction research for the founder's "make it look cool" brief)

Founder's brief, 2026-11-20: professional, futuristic, vibrant, high quality icons, a nice text font,
"wow, this app looks cool". The lines below are what to take; none is to be copied (no logos, marks,
artwork or recognisable layouts).

## New this refresh (2026-11-20)

- 2026-06-23 · Ypulse: brands in fashion, fintech and gaming now ask agencies for dark mode and "sleek,
  high-contrast designs" for Gen Z; unclear if trend or comfort. [Ypulse](https://www.ypulse.com/newsfeed/2026/06/23/gen-z-demand-is-forcing-brands-to-include-dark-mode-features-on-their-apps/).
  Take: keep the dark base and push the contrast and polish, never go pale or flat. Strength: moderate.
- 2025-06 to 2026-03 · Apple Liquid Glass (iOS 26): translucent material, praised as fresh, criticised for
  legibility; Apple cut transparency in later betas and says it stays. [TechCrunch 2025-06-10](https://techcrunch.com/2025/06/10/love-it-or-hate-it-apples-new-liquid-glass-design-is-getting-mixed-reviews),
  [MacStories](https://www.macstories.net/links/opinionated-design-early-reactions-to-apples-liquid-glass-design-language/),
  [AppleInsider 2026-03-26](https://appleinsider.com/articles/26/03/26/stop-holding-out-hope-liquid-glass-will-be-mandatory-in-ios-27).
  Take: frosted depth looks premium to teens already living in iOS 26, but text sits on a plate that is
  at least 70% opaque and is measured (Direction C). Strength: moderate.
- 2026-04-09 · NBC's NBA graphics: bold, "ownable", flat colour (no gradients or layered effects), a
  medallion scorebug, snappy animation taken from dribble rhythm and crossovers.
  [SVG](https://www.sportsvideo.org/2026/04/09/newstalgia-an-in-depth-look-at-how-the-return-of-the-inba-on-nbc-i-was-driven-by-a-bold-and-ownable-graphics-package/).
  Take: a scorebug plate and quick, rhythmic motion are the language of "this is a game" (Direction A).
  Strength: moderate (trade press, interview).
- 2025-09 to 2026-06 · NBA on Prime: an LED, "video game-like" court, in-house graphics, viewer-built
  multi-game layouts; won a Sports Emmy in its first season.
  [SVG 2026-06-04](https://www.sportsvideo.org/2026/06/04/prime-video-caps-first-nba-season-with-sports-emmy-win-carries-in-house-production-into-wnba-campaign/),
  [The Big Lead](https://www.thebiglead.com/?p=96371). Take: glowing court lines as a brand device,
  like our own viewfinder brackets (Direction B). Strength: moderate.
- 2025-12 · Strava Year in Sport (built with Rive): a motion system "fluid, energetic", a "premium feel"
  for subscribers, a vignette per athlete, 2.6M shares of the 2024 edition. [Rive](https://rive.app/blog/strava-year-in-sport-built-with-rive).
  Take: the result moment is the share moment; give it motion and one giant number. Strength: moderate.
- 2026-04-03 and 2025-10 · WHOOP added shareable activity graphics (April 2026); its home screen made the
  "+" button bigger and central. [WHOOP](https://www.whoop.com/us/en/thelocker/2026-whats-new/),
  [the5krunner](https://the5krunner.com/2025/10/15/whoop-homescreen-gets-a-revamp/). Take: a share
  graphic per activity is standard now; the main action should be the heaviest thing on screen. Strength: moderate.
- Undated (patch 5.08 era) · Riot's Valorant interface preview: raised contrast, "pushed" colour values,
  simpler shape language, motion design "to boost game flow and expression".
  [Riot](https://playvalorant.com/en-gb/news/game-updates/preview-the-future-of-valorant-s-interface/).
  Take: game menus got more vivid and more consistent in shape, not plainer. Third-party analysis
  (practitioner, weak) says Valorant pairs a condensed all-caps display face with a plain sans body: a
  scoreboard-style pairing (Direction A). Strength: strong for Riot's own words, weak for the fonts.
- 2025-09 · EA Sports FC 26 rebuilt its menus around a personal "For You" hub; reviewers split, calling the
  touchscreen-style UI clunky for some modes. [RealSport101](https://realsport101.com/article/fc-26s-new-ui-a-deep-dive-into-the-redesigned-ultimate-team-menu),
  [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/sports/ea-sports-fc-26-review). Take: a personal Today hub is
  fine; keep one tap to the thing the player came for. Strength: moderate.
- 2025-09 · NBA 2K26 reviews praise cleaner menus, while one says it "could use more color".
  [Vice](https://www.vice.com/en/article/nba-2k26-is-a-great-game-when-it-focuses-on-the-basketball-review),
  [Gaming Trend](https://gamingtrend.com/reviews/nba-2k26-review-nothing-but-net/). Take: clean alone is
  not enough; colour and polish are what players notice (the founder's own note). Strength: practitioner.
- Not found, so not claimed: a 2026 Nike Training Club or Hudl redesign, a Strava font or dark-mode
  change, any teen study of gradients and glow. The 2026 "Gen Z UI" roundups (muz.li, moburst, lyssna)
  say restraint and usability, and one cites an unverified "73% prefer dark mode": weak, not used.

## Kept from 2026-11-11 (still current)

- 2026-11 · Nike's 2025 youth campaign: grainy, high-contrast, handheld, animated type, showed the miss
  before the make. Take: raw phone footage, hard contrast, moving type; show the miss, then the make.
- 2026-11 · NBA 2025-26 City Edition: black bases, one strong accent. Take: black plus one accent is
  current; local identity comes from Irish places, never NBA city looks.
- 2026-11 · Teens meet basketball through clips and individual players. Take: every result screen
  should survive a screen recording.
- 2026-11 · Sports media house style: heavy sans headline over a real photo. Take: a heavy headline over
  the player's own camera frame, never a cut-out pro.
- 2026-11 · Overtime: named things players belong to. Take: name levels, drills and challenges (our badges
  already do: "Heating Up", "Week Warrior", "Locked In").
- 2026-11 · 2K's player card; WHOOP's three dials; Strava recap. Take: a personal card, three headline
  stats on Today, a free season recap.
- 2026-11 · Canva 2026 "Imperfect by Design" (grain, collage). Take: share cards and social only.
- Avoid: emerald and shamrock (reads as Basketball Ireland), red-versus-green only for MAKE/MISS, a dying
  flame for a missed day, text straight on thin glass, a gradient on text that has to be read.
