## Bugs

### B3 · Landing demo stays on "Starting…" for ever when the clip download fails · P2
Steps: signed out, 390x844, open `/`, scroll to "Watch it call real shots", lose the
connection (or the download drops), tap "Play the sample".
Expected: a message such as "Couldn't load the clip" with a retry, or it starts once the
connection is back. Actual: black video box and "Starting…" with a spinner; no message after
60 s; going back online does not recover it (60 s watched); only a page reload does.
Repeats: 6 of 6 (offline 4 of 4, connection reset 1 of 1, offline then back online 1 of 1).
Console: `GET /samples/tracker-sample.mp4` net::ERR_INTERNET_DISCONNECTED (or
ERR_CONNECTION_RESET), twice; no page error. Screenshot: `shots/2026-11-16-b3-demo-stuck-starting.png`.
Likely: `downloadSample()` (`src/lib/video/sampleClip.ts` 35-41) turns a failed fetch into
the plain URL and keeps that for the page; the video then fails to load and nothing in
`startSample()` (`ShotTracker.tsx` ~1258) or the calibrate step handles the video's error,
so "Starting…" (line 1686) never ends. Confirm with a video `error` listener. Likely also hits
`/welcome/sample` (step 2 of setup, same component) for a signed-in player: confirm locally.
Rank: P2, not P1: only after a failed download, and a reload gets round it. A gym with weak
signal is the real case, and it is the first thing the landing page asks a visitor to try.

Screenshot: `shots/2026-11-16-b4-signup-invalid-input.png` (text typed by the tester).
