Expected: the clip starts within a few seconds. Actual: "Loading the sample…" for about
15 s (Resource Timing: `tracker-sample.mp4` 200, 14.6 s, 2.9 MB transferred), then "Play
the sample". Repeats: 1 of 1 measured; runs 2 and 3 never finished within 60 s but the

per its comment). Confirm on a phone on mobile data; if it repeats, a progress figure or a
smaller clip would help a new player on step 2 of 4.

### B2 · Today's main buttons are 32px tall and are buttons inside links · P3
Steps: tester, 390px, `/dashboard`, the "Log your first shots" card.
Actual: "Log shots" 79x32 and "Track with the camera" 150x32 (`Button size="sm"`, `h-8`),
each a `<button>` nested in a `<a>` (`src/app/(app)/dashboard/page.tsx` lines 242-247).
Expected: main actions at least 44px (our checklist); one focusable element per action.
Repeats: 2 of 2 (DOM read twice). Nested interactive elements give keyboard users two tab
stops per action and are invalid HTML. Above the 24px WCAG 2.2 minimum, so P3.
