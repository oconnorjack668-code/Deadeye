# Video references

Strong short videos from sports, fitness and training apps and from hooper
creators, with notes on why each works and what Deadeye can borrow. The video
editor picks 3 to 5 from here for each brief (playbook step 2). Started
2026-11-11, empty on purpose: an entry goes in only when someone has opened
its link and watched it, so nothing here is from memory or a summary.

## How to add an entry

1. Find it: TikTok Creative Center's Top Ads
   (https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/inspiration/topads/pc/en,
   filters for region, objective, language and format; checked 2026-11-11),
   Meta's Ad Library (https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/; refused automated
   reading on 2026-11-11, so open it in the founder's browser), the accounts
   of sports apps and shooting coaches, or a post the founder sends.
2. Watch it in full, twice: once with sound, once without.
3. Copy the template below, newest first. Use the post's own link. Describe
   it in your own words; never download or copy the video, its music or its
   text into the repo or a project.
4. Leave out anyone's personal details. If the video shows someone who looks
   under 18, note only the format, never who they are.
5. Remove an entry when its link dies or a better example of the same thing
   is added.

## What to note (and why)

- **Hook (0 to 2 s):** what is on frame one, what is said or written. The
  first seconds decide the video (findings: "Pacing and retention").
- **Length and cut rate:** total seconds, roughly how many cuts, where the
  longest hold is.
- **Captions:** spoken or read-alone, size, where on screen, how they move.
- **Sound:** voice (real or synthetic), music feel, effects, silence.
- **Product moment:** when the app or product is first seen, and how.
- **Ending:** the action asked for, and whether it is said and shown.
- **Borrow:** the one thing Deadeye could take, within our rules
  (`docs/agent-os/knowledge/video-editor.md`).
- **Avoid:** anything that breaks our rules (overpromising, pressure, faces of
  minors, licensed music we can't use).
