## Checklist

### Every draft

- [ ] To an adult in a role (organiser, coach, parent group admin, adult creator) or a public
      community post; never a cold email, text or DM to a young person (S.I. 336/2011 reg. 13;
      DPC: only 16+ or a parent can consent to electronic marketing; under-18s' best interests).
- [ ] Community posts come from the founder's own named account and say plainly they built
      the app; the community's self-promotion rule quoted or linked; weekly thread or
      moderators asked where required.
- [ ] Gives something useful first; under 150 words; one ask; never the same text twice; at
      most one community a day; no DMs to members of a server or group.
- [ ] Its own tracking link or QR per route (`?ref=` or UTM); no per-person tracking.
- [ ] Only features that exist and prices on `/pro`; no "thousands of players", no results an
      ordinary child cannot reach, no pressure to recruit friends (ASAI section 7, CAP 5.2.2).
- [ ] The founder sends or posts; nothing published by the agent.

### Creators and partners

- [ ] Creator clearly an adult, contacted by their public business address; recorded by type.
- [ ] Any deal, free Pro included, labelled `#Ad` (or `#Fógra`) at the very start of the post
      (CCPC/ASAI, ASA/CAP/CMA influencer guidance); the decision is the founder's.
- [ ] Events and camps through organisers (Basketball Ireland 3x3, National Camps for ages 12
      to 17, local camps); permission asked; no data collected and no photos of children
      without parent consent.

### Referrals

- [ ] The player passes on a link or code themselves; no contact-book upload, no messages
      sent on their behalf; non-cash, in-app rewards only; referral totals never shown as a
      per-child league table; safety-reviewer before the founder.

### Reading results

- [ ] Judged on first sessions within 7 days; under about 30 players a hint, not a result.
- [ ] Zero from a small number is not proof of zero (rule of three: 0 of 30 could still be up
      to about 10%).
- [ ] One or two routes run for several weeks before adding more.
- [ ] `?ref=` is saved at sign-up and counted by route (joined, 1st session) at `/admin/funnel`
      and in the weekly counts file once PR #97 merges (2026-11-13); check it is merged before
      reading route numbers. Links go to `/` or `/signup` only, with a plain code (`a-z0-9-_`,
      max 40). Radio, print and word of mouth show as "no route".
- [ ] Optional "how did you hear about us?" with fixed answers could still catch the "no
      route" players; suggest it to the lead, not as a ref-capture item.

### Before drafting

- [ ] Trend board (`trends/ads.md`) read; if over 7 days old or "never", say so in the plan.
- [ ] Clubs, schools and academies (and their adult or social teams) are club-growth's; check
      `clubs/pipeline.md` before adding a route.
- [ ] The founder is under 18 (`docs/launch-checklist.md`): any press, interview or email to an
      organisation copies in a parent, and a parent agrees before the founder's name or photo
      appears anywhere.
