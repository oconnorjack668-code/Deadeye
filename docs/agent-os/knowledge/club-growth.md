## Checklist

### Outreach drafts

- [ ] Addressed to the club's public channel or a role (secretary, chair, underage
      coordinator, DSCO); never a private person's name or details.
- [ ] 50 to 125 words, plain words, one ask (a short chat, or one coach trying one team),
      at most two questions; a short subject line naming the club, town or age group.
- [ ] Opens with a real local reason (players nearby, the area board, Wexford results
      once there are some). Warm route looked for first.
- [ ] Safeguarding stated exactly as built: head coaches 18+ and confirm Garda vetting; no
      private messages, coaches post to the whole team; under-16s need a parent's approval
      and those parents see coach posts. Never says or hints that BI approves Deadeye.
- [ ] Features and prices checked against the app, `/pro` and
      `docs/sales/deadeye-club-plans.html`; founding offer quoted whole (first 3 clubs,
      4+ teams, free to 31 Jan 2027, then €59.99 a team a season, closes 31 Dec 2026).
- [ ] No pressure, no "everyone else has signed up", no fake deadlines, no rival named.
- [ ] Follow-up planned: at most two, about a week apart, then stop. The founder sends.
- [ ] Timing checked against the season calendar below.

### Trial plans

- [ ] One team first: head coach as champion, a committee contact (chair or underage
      coordinator) who knows it is running, and the DSCO told in writing before players join.
- [ ] Two or three success criteria readable from the Monday counts, and a decision date.
- [ ] Written: the plan and `/pro` price if it works, a clean stop if it does not.
- [ ] Parent message for under-16s ready, ideally handed out where parents are present.
- [ ] No exports, club-wide view of children's data or sharing with a school promised;
      any such request goes to the founder.
- [ ] Adds almost no admin for the coach; the founder offers to help set teams up.
