## Lessons from the founder

Newest first. The 2026-10 ones come from the first promo's handoffs
(`2026-10-20-promo-video-and-coach-format.md`, `2026-10-21-promo-rim-line-fix.md`).

- 2026-11-09 · On the first tracker cut, the founder wanted the logo on the end
  card, no orange underline under DEADEYE, and the far, low footage too small.
  **Rule:** end cards carry the logo (`public/logo.png`) in the app's own colours
  (zinc-950 background, white heavy numbers, zinc grey secondary, orange only for
  the one action; no new colours, no underline); zoom far or low footage so the
  shot and hoop fill the frame, keeping the app's count readable.
- 2026-11-09 · A teen-viewer critique of the second cut (founder: apply all fixes).
  **Rules:** no digital silence (synthesised room tone, a tick on each count change);
  frame one moves; the voice never narrates an event before it happens; check a crop
  keeps the app's MAKE/MISS words whole; end on the viewer's action, numbers small.
- 2026-11-09 · The founder thinks the tracker's make/miss and streak visuals look
  unpolished (the flame covers the number). **Rule:** until the redesign ships,
  never make the flame or MAKE/MISS banners the hero; lean on the real shot, the
  ball going in, the numbers and the slow-mo replay.
- 2026-11-09 · Founder: an AI voice-over is allowed, "whatever is best". **Rule:**
  decide on the evidence; if used, short, word for word with the captions, AI label on.
- 2026-11-09 · A plan called reminders broken from an old handoff's "Open" list.
  **Rule:** "Open" items are to-dos; check or say "unconfirmed", never "broken".
- 2026-11-07 · The first promo took too many rounds of small fixes. **Rule:**
  check the whole video frame by frame against the checklist; show a finished cut.
- 2026-10-21 · The rim line ran past the ring onto the bracket. **Rule:** every
  overlay is checked at full size on stills (flash, shake, zoom frames too).
- 2026-10-20 · Captions drifted from the voice; darkened patches hid footage.
  **Rules:** one voice clip per phrase, timed to its caption; never darken or blur a block.
- 2026-10-20 · "Any angle" and "160+ drills" were wrong. **Rule:** every claim is checked in the code.
- 2026-10-20 · Shown side by side, the founder preferred the first promo's
  look to a new 32s build. **Rule:** before rebuilding a whole video, show
  one short sample of a new look next to the current one.
- 2026-10-20 · "Can you not add X" from the founder meant "add X". **Rule:**
  when a request could be read two ways, say which reading you took.
- 2026-10-23 · Words that promise too much: "never misses", "100% accurate",
  "calls every shot", "counts every make", "go pro", "last chance", "only X
  left". **Rule:** none of these, anywhere.

## Checklist before the founder sees a video or post

- [ ] Hook in the first 2 seconds says what the viewer gets; the app is named
      or shown early.
- [ ] Every on-screen number and claim has a source in the app or code; any
      "this doesn't work yet" is checked as current, not taken from an old handoff.
- [ ] Every overlay lands on the real object on still frames, including fast
      frames (flashes, shakes, zooms).
- [ ] Captions match the voice word for word and change with it.
- [ ] No darkened or blurred patches; no leftover old captions or watermarks.
- [ ] Readable on a phone: large enough at arm's length, on long enough to read twice.
- [ ] Works with the sound off (captions carry the message).
- [ ] No identifiable under-18 without a parent's written consent; app
      screens from the tester account.
- [ ] Ends on one clear action that matches the activation goal (try the
      demo, log a first session).
- [ ] Watched once more start to finish at real speed on a phone-sized
      window.

From the research (the tag names the section below that backs each line):

- [ ] Frame one is action (a shot going up, the count ticking), never a logo,
      black card or slow setup; the app is on screen by 3s. [Hooks]
- [ ] The brand is in the scene (app on the phone, the name said aloud), in
      short repeated moments; any logo sting is under 1s and after the hook;
      no corner logo overlay. [Branding]
- [ ] Caption timing: at most 17 characters a second, each caption on for at
      least 0.8s, in within 1 to 2 frames of its words, out about 0.5s after,
      no caption hanging over a cut. [Captions]
- [ ] Text and the call to action sit inside x 140-940, y 270-1250 on a
      1080x1920 frame (Meta's 14/35/6% plus TikTok's right-hand buttons);
      checked against TikTok's template for the caption length used. [Captions]
- [ ] Made for sound on (voice, swish, count tick) and still makes sense
      muted. [Captions]
- [ ] Looks like a post from a real player, not a TV ad: real court, real
      phone screen, hand-held is fine. [People]
- [ ] Shows only what an ordinary player can get; states what they need (a
      hoop, a phone propped 3 to 6 m back, off to one side). [Rules]
- [ ] No pressure: nothing that says a player is behind or "not serious"
      without it, no "ask your parents", no "only" or "just" before a price.
      [Rules]
- [ ] The last line is one action, said and shown, in the same words as the
      first screen of the page it links to; "link in bio" only once the bio
      link is confirmed live on that account. [Sign-ups]
- [ ] Music only from TikTok's Commercial Music Library or Meta's Sound
      Collection, and a TikTok library track is never reused on Instagram.
      [Rules]
- [ ] Labels: TikTok commercial content setting on; #Ad on any post where
      someone is given something to post; AI label on if any voice or footage
      is synthetic and realistic. [Rules]
- [ ] One video, three openings: give the founder 3 different first-3-second
      versions to choose from or test. [Hooks]
