## Lessons from the founder

Newest first. Each one cost the founder a round of fixes on the first promo
(handoffs `2026-10-20-promo-video-and-coach-format.md`,
`2026-10-21-promo-rim-line-fix.md`, `marketing/2026-10-23-first-promo.md`).

- 2026-11-07 · The first promo took too many rounds of small fixes. **Rule:**
  check the whole video yourself, frame by frame where things move, against
  the checklist below before the founder sees it. Show the founder a finished
  cut, not a draft for them to proofread.
- 2026-10-21 · The orange rim line on the first hoop ran past the ring onto
  the bracket; the founder spotted it. **Rule:** every overlay (rim line, dot,
  ring, score badge) is checked at full size on still frames, including flash,
  shake and zoom frames, and ends exactly where the real object is.
- 2026-10-20 · Captions drifted from the voice. **Rule:** one voice clip per
  phrase, each caption timed to its own words; check sync at every caption
  change.
- 2026-10-20 · The founder disliked darkened patches over footage. **Rule:**
  never darken or blur a block of the frame to hide something; cover with a
  clean patch from a nearby moment, or re-cut.
- 2026-10-20 · "Any angle" was on screen, but the tracker needs the phone off
  to one side. Drills were briefly "160+" when the app has 150 (160+ is gym
  exercises). **Rule:** every claim and number on screen or in a caption is
  checked against the app or the code, with its source written in the plan
  (as `docs/brand/promo-video/PLAN.md` does).
- 2026-10-20 · Shown side by side, the founder preferred the first promo's
  look to a new 32s build. **Rule:** before rebuilding a whole video, show
  one short sample of a new look next to the current one.
- 2026-10-20 · "Can you not add X" from the founder meant "add X". **Rule:**
  when a request could be read two ways, say which reading you took.
- 2026-10-23 · Words that promise too much: "never misses", "100% accurate",
  "calls every shot", "counts every make", "go pro", "last chance", "only X
  left". **Rule:** none of these, anywhere.

## Checklist before the founder sees a video or post

- [ ] Hook in the first 2 seconds says what the viewer gets; the app is named
      or shown early.
- [ ] Every on-screen number and claim has a source in the app or code.
- [ ] Every overlay lands on the real object on still frames, including fast
      frames (flashes, shakes, zooms).
- [ ] Captions match the voice word for word and change with it.
- [ ] No darkened or blurred patches; no leftover old captions or watermarks.
- [ ] Readable on a phone: captions inside the safe zone (clear of the
      TikTok and Reels buttons and caption area), large enough to read at
      arm's length, on screen long enough to read twice.
- [ ] Works with the sound off (captions carry the message).
- [ ] No identifiable under-18 without a parent's written consent; app
      screens from the tester account.
- [ ] Ends on one clear action that matches the activation goal (try the
      demo, log a first session).
- [ ] Watched once more start to finish at real speed on a phone-sized
      window.

From the research (the tag names the section below that backs each line):

- [ ] Frame one is action (a shot going up, the count ticking), never a logo,
      black card or slow setup; the app is on screen by 3s. [Hooks]
- [ ] The brand is in the scene (app on the phone, the name said aloud), in
      short repeated moments; any logo sting is under 1s and after the hook;
      no corner logo overlay. [Branding]
- [ ] Caption timing: at most 17 characters a second, each caption on for at
      least 0.8s, in within 1 to 2 frames of its words, out about 0.5s after,
      no caption hanging over a cut. [Captions]
- [ ] Text and the call to action sit inside the safe zone: top 14%, bottom
      35% and 6% each side kept clear (Meta); checked against TikTok's
      template for the caption length used. [Captions]
- [ ] Made for sound on (voice, swish, count tick) and still makes sense
      muted. [Captions]
- [ ] Looks like a post from a real player, not a TV ad: real court, real
      phone screen, hand-held is fine. [People]
- [ ] Shows only what an ordinary player can get; states what they need (a
      hoop, a phone propped 3 to 6 m back, off to one side). [Rules]
- [ ] No pressure: nothing that says a player is behind or "not serious"
      without it, no "ask your parents", no "only" or "just" before a price.
      [Rules]
- [ ] The last line is one action, said and shown, in the same words as the
      first screen of the page it links to. [Sign-ups]
- [ ] Music only from TikTok's Commercial Music Library or Meta's Sound
      Collection, and a TikTok library track is never reused on Instagram.
      [Rules]
- [ ] Labels: TikTok commercial content setting on; #Ad on any post where
      someone is given something to post; AI label on if any voice or footage
      is synthetic and realistic. [Rules]
- [ ] One video, three openings: give the founder 3 different first-3-second
      versions to choose from or test. [Hooks]

## What the research says

Strength: study (peer-reviewed or large survey), official (platform or
regulator), industry (method stated, often platform-paid), practitioner.

### Hooks and the first seconds

- About half of a TikTok ad's effect lands in the first 2s, and the first 6s carry about 90% of its ad recall. **Deadeye:** the opening 2s decide the video; no logo or setup first. [TikTok Marketing Science via Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/tiktok-shares-new-notes-maximize-ad-effectiveness/694989/), 2023, official.
- TikTok: give the proposition in the first 3s, hook within 6s through surprise or emotion, keep a DIY, not over-polished look, end with a clear call to action, run 3 to 5 versions. **Deadeye:** matches the recipe below. [TikTok creative best practices](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/creative-best-practices?lang=en), 2025, official.
- Google, for app ads: show the app in use within the first 2 to 3s, hook in 5s, lead with the benefit and show how to use it. **Deadeye:** the live count on a real shot is our 2s proof. [Google Grow My App](https://ads.google.cn/home/grow-my-app/take-action/), [checklist](https://services.google.com/fh/files/misc/creative_best_practices_checklist.pdf), 2024, official.
- Eye-tracking and zapping data: surprise grabs attention, joy keeps people watching. **Deadeye:** open on a surprise (the phone calls the shot), keep the joy (makes, numbers going up). [Teixeira, Wedel and Pieters, JMR](https://research.tilburguniversity.edu/en/publications/emotion-induced-engagement-in-internet-video-ads/), 2012, study (28 ads, 50 viewers).

### Branding

- 887 TikTok ads, 92,000 viewers: a sound cue in the first 2s gave the biggest awareness lift (+191%), the product shown in the action +182%; logo overlays reduced it; creator-led ads held 39% more attention. **Deadeye:** make the swish and count tick our sound; show the app in hand, not a corner logo. [System1 and TikTok via Chief Marketer](https://chiefmarketer.com/system1-tiktok-study-branding-elements-and-creator-led-content-improve-short-form-video-ads/), 2025, industry.
- One long, central brand shot made viewers skip more; the same brand time in short repeated bursts cut skipping. **Deadeye:** keep the logo sting short and spread the name through the video. [Teixeira, Wedel and Pieters, Marketing Science](https://research.tilburguniversity.edu/en/publications/moment-to-moment-optimal-branding-in-tv-commercials-preventing-av/), 2010, study (TV ads).
- Google's ABCD: brand early and often, seen and heard; for Shorts, real people using the product, a fun tone, tight framing, a clear call to action. **Deadeye:** say "Deadeye" aloud, not just on the end card. [Google Ads help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/14783551), 2024 (Kantar data 2021), official.

### Sound, captions, text and framing

- Reels play with sound on by default and over 75% of Reels views are sound on; 9:16 video with audio and key messages in the safe zone got about 2x more Reels delivery. **Deadeye:** design for sound, caption for silence. [Meta for Developers](https://developers.facebook.com/blog/post/2024/11/07/unlock-the-power-of-reel-ads/), [Meta Reels ads](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/facebook-instagram-reels-ads), 2024, official.
- Meta's safe zone keeps about 14% at the top, 35% at the bottom and 6% each side clear of text and logos; TikTok's safe zone shrinks as the post caption gets longer. **Deadeye:** keep captions short and check both templates. [Meta ads guide](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide/update/image/instagram-reels), [TikTok in-feed specs](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/tiktok-auction-in-feed-ads), 2025, official.
- Subtitle standards: children's programmes at most 17 characters a second, each subtitle at least 0.8s, starting within 1 to 2 frames of the speech, ending about 0.5s after, with set rules at shot changes. TikTok's "5 to 10 words a second" is far faster; we use the slower rule for 13-year-olds. [Netflix style guide](https://partnerhelp.netflixstudios.com/hc/en-us/articles/217350977-English-USA-Timed-Text-Style-Guide), [timing](https://partnerhelp.netflixstudios.com/hc/en-us/articles/360051554394-Timed-Text-Style-Guide-Subtitle-Timing-Guidelines), 2025, official (broadcast standard).
- Google: don't rely on music; the video must work with sound off; voice-over is recommended; keep text out of the top 10% and bottom 25%. [Google checklist](https://services.google.com/fh/files/misc/creative_best_practices_checklist.pdf), 2024, official.

### Length

- TikTok: longer videos reach fewer people and most of the effect comes early, so shorter native videos are best for reach. Google: 10 to 30s, try several lengths. **Deadeye:** aim for 15 to 25s; cut anything that is not a hook, proof or call to action. [TikTok via Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/tiktok-shares-new-notes-maximize-ad-effectiveness/694989/), 2023, official; [Google Grow My App](https://ads.google.cn/home/grow-my-app/take-action/), official.

### People, authenticity and young audiences

- TikTok: real people in the video added 9% watch time, a creative story 16%. **Deadeye:** a real player and a small story (a session, a score to beat) beat a feature list. [TikTok via Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/tiktok-shares-new-notes-maximize-ad-effectiveness/694989/), 2023, official.
- People identify with and trust social creators more than celebrities, and feeling similar drives it. **Deadeye:** an ordinary player like the viewer, not a star. [Schouten, Janssen and Verspaget, Int. J. of Advertising](https://repository.tilburguniversity.edu/items/c1168b1a-fb6d-4bbd-884c-e11fb4e0e9e9), 2020, study (Dutch adults).
- "Underdog" stories (a disadvantage plus effort and drive) raised buying intent and loyalty because people saw themselves in them. **Deadeye:** "Aim is earned" is an effort story; show the reps, not talent. [Paharia et al., J. of Consumer Research](https://ideas.repec.org/a/oup/jconrs/doi10.1086-656219.html), 2011, study (4 experiments).
- 1,000 Australians aged 14 to 29: 40% trust a brand less when ads are over-polished; they want authentic content (56%) and to learn something new (50%); creators are the most trusted source (41%). [YouthInsight via Mediaweek](https://www.mediaweek.com.au/advertising/i-hate-ads-study-lifts-lid-on-what-gen-z-love-and-hate-about-brands), 2025, industry.
- Teens aged 12 to 16 accept sponsored videos but push back when the ad part breaks the video's flow; a clear disclosure helps girls spot ads but does not make them more critical. **Deadeye:** be useful first and honest always; teens will not always defend themselves. [Van Dam and van Reijmersdal](https://cyberpsychology.eu/article/view/11976), 2019, study (20 teens); [Balaban et al.](https://www.cogitatiopress.com/mediaandcommunication/article/view/4652), 2022, study (241 girls).
- US teens: YouTube 92%, TikTok 68%, Instagram 63%; about 1 in 5 on TikTok or YouTube "almost constantly". **Deadeye:** the same 9:16 cut could go on YouTube Shorts (founder decision). [Pew Research](https://www.pewresearch.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/20/2025/12/PI_2025.12.09_Teens-Social-Media-AI_REPORT.pdf), 2025, study (1,458 teens).
- About 7 in 10 Irish 8 to 12-year-olds have an account on a 13+ platform. **Deadeye:** under-13s will see our posts; write every one as if a 10-year-old is watching. [CyberSafeKids via Irish Times](https://www.irishtimes.com/technology/big-tech/2026/09/02/measures-to-keep-young-children-from-accessing-online-platforms-failing-says-cybersafekids/), 2026, study (Irish survey).

### Sport

- 7,000 sports-content viewers aged 18 to 49 in 7 countries: learning something is a top reason to watch sport on TikTok; fans there share by DM, stitch and join challenges more; women are 1.4x more likely to pick TikTok as their top sports platform. **Deadeye:** teach one thing per video, make it worth sending to a teammate, run challenges, make content for girls too. [TikTok and Ipsos](https://www.ipsos.com/sites/default/files/ct/news/documents/2026-05/TikTok%20x%20Ipsos%20-%20The%20New%20Sporting%20Era.pdf), 2025, industry (platform-paid).
- Sport-linked ads scored above average on emotion in about half of 170 campaigns (33,000 people), mainly through happiness. **Deadeye:** the joy of a make and of training with mates, not fear of falling behind. [System1 and FUSE via Advanced Television](https://www.advanced-television.com/2023/08/01/study-sport-sponsorship-can-be-stronger-than-traditional-ads/), 2023, industry.

### From a view to a sign-up

- Google: the call to action should mirror the user's goal and use the same words as the app listing. **Deadeye:** end-card words match the landing page's first screen ("Try the demo"). [Google checklist](https://services.google.com/fh/files/misc/creative_best_practices_checklist.pdf), 2024, official.
- Meta: hook early in the safe zone, call to action at the end in the safe zone. [Meta for Developers](https://developers.facebook.com/blog/post/2024/11/07/unlock-the-power-of-reel-ads/), 2024, official.
- Ads that reuse the landing page's picture and words convert better in many A/B tests (e.g. +47.6% in one), but none is peer-reviewed. **Deadeye:** the demo the video promises is the first thing on the page. [NextAfter](https://www.nextafter.com/?p=13009), practitioner.
- Instagram's top ranking signals are watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach; sends matter most for reaching non-followers. **Deadeye:** "send this to your shooting partner" fits; ask once, gently. [Mosseri via Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/), 2025, official (statement).

### Rules

- ASAI Code section 7 (children): no making a child feel inferior or less loyal for not having it, no urging them to ask parents, no exaggerating what an ordinary child can achieve, say what else is needed, no "only" or "just" before a price. [ASAI 7.5 and 7.6](https://adstandards.ie/code/children/), 2021 revision, official.
- ASAI 4.9 and 4.10: hold written proof for every factual claim before publishing, covering the overall impression; 3.31 to 3.34: it must be clear it is marketing and who it is from. [ASAI section 4](https://adstandards.ie/code/misleading-advertising/), [general rules](https://adstandards.ie/code/general-rules/), official.
- CCPC and ASAI: label all commercial content #Ad (or #Fógra), visible at once, including gifted items and own brands. **Deadeye:** any player or creator given free Pro to post uses #Ad. [CCPC](https://www.ccpc.ie/news-and-media/news/article/2023/10/26/if-in-doubt--label-it!---new-guidance-on-social-media-advertising-for-influencers-issued-by-ccpc-and-asai), 2023, official.
- TikTok: switch on the content disclosure setting when promoting a brand, product or service; in TikTok's test of about 2 million videos, disclosure made no difference to performance. [TikTok help](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/about-the-content-disclosure-setting-for-creators), 2026, official.
- Coimisiún na Meán's Online Safety Code makes platforms offer a way to declare commercial content; use it. [Matheson Q&A](https://www.matheson.com/insights/detail/the-online-safety-code--q-a), 2024, official (via law firm).
- TikTok's Commercial Music Library is for business use on TikTok only; other TikTok sounds carry no business rights. Meta offers its Sound Collection for business Reels. [TikTok CML terms](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/global/commercial-music-library-user-terms/en), [Meta Reels ads](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/facebook-instagram-reels-ads), official.
- Meta requires a label on realistic AI-made or altered audio or video (a realistic AI voice-over counts); TikTok requires a label on realistic AI content. [Meta](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/02/labeling-ai-generated-images-on-facebook-instagram-and-threads/), 2024 (updated 2025); [TikTok](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/new-labels-for-disclosing-ai-generated-content?lang=en), 2023, official.
- The same ad labelled "AI-generated" was rated less credible, less emotional and less memorable, and got fewer clicks. **Deadeye:** prefer a real voice (a consenting adult's, or the founder's with a parent's OK); if synthetic, label it. [NIM](https://www.nim.org/en/publications/detail/transparency-alone-does-not-create-trust), 2025, study (298 adults).

## Promo recipe for Deadeye (15 to 30s, 9:16)

| Time | Beat | Backed by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 to 2s | Real shot goes up, the live count ticks: the surprise. Voice and caption give the benefit in 6 words or fewer. Swish and tick as the sound cue. | TikTok 2s and 6s; Teixeira 2012; System1 |
| 2 to 5s | Name it: "Deadeye" said aloud, the app on the phone in shot. A logo sting, if any, under 1s. | Google 2 to 3s; ABCD; Teixeira 2010 |
| 5 to 18s | Proof, one idea per beat: the honest setup line, the live count, the results screen, one thing learnt. Real player (adult, or consented), hand-held. | Google "show how"; Shorts ABCD; ASAI 7.6 |
| 18 to 24s | Pay-off: a make, a number going up, the effort line ("Aim is earned"). Joy, not pressure. | Teixeira 2012; Paharia; System1 and FUSE |
| last 3 to 4s | One action, said and shown in the safe zone, in the landing page's own words: "Try the demo. No sign-up." plus the address. | Meta; Google; message match |

Make three versions of 0 to 3s on the same body. Aim for 15 to 25s overall.
Square cuts reuse the timeline but recheck every caption and overlay.
