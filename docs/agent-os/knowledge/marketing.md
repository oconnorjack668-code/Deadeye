## Lessons from the founder

Newest first. Each one cost the founder a round of fixes on the first promo
(handoffs `2026-10-20-promo-video-and-coach-format.md`,
`2026-10-21-promo-rim-line-fix.md`, `marketing/2026-10-23-first-promo.md`).

- 2026-11-07 · The first promo took too many rounds of small fixes. **Rule:**
  check the whole video yourself, frame by frame where things move, against
  the checklist below before the founder sees it. Show the founder a finished
  cut, not a draft for them to proofread.
- 2026-10-21 · The orange rim line on the first hoop ran past the ring onto
  the bracket; the founder spotted it. **Rule:** every overlay (rim line, dot,
  ring, score badge) is checked at full size on still frames, including flash,
  shake and zoom frames, and ends exactly where the real object is.
- 2026-10-20 · Captions drifted from the voice. **Rule:** one voice clip per
  phrase, each caption timed to its own words; check sync at every caption
  change.
- 2026-10-20 · The founder disliked darkened patches over footage. **Rule:**
  never darken or blur a block of the frame to hide something; cover with a
  clean patch from a nearby moment, or re-cut.
- 2026-10-20 · "Any angle" was on screen, but the tracker needs the phone off
  to one side. Drills were briefly "160+" when the app has 150 (160+ is gym
  exercises). **Rule:** every claim and number on screen or in a caption is
  checked against the app or the code, with its source written in the plan
  (as `docs/brand/promo-video/PLAN.md` does).
- 2026-10-20 · Shown side by side, the founder preferred the first promo's
  look to a new 32s build. **Rule:** before rebuilding a whole video, show
  one short sample of a new look next to the current one.
- 2026-10-20 · "Can you not add X" from the founder meant "add X". **Rule:**
  when a request could be read two ways, say which reading you took.
- 2026-10-23 · Words that promise too much: "never misses", "100% accurate",
  "calls every shot", "counts every make", "go pro", "last chance", "only X
  left". **Rule:** none of these, anywhere.

## Checklist before the founder sees a video or post

- [ ] Hook in the first 2 seconds says what the viewer gets; the app is named
      or shown early.
- [ ] Every on-screen number and claim has a source in the app or code.
- [ ] Every overlay lands on the real object on still frames, including fast
      frames (flashes, shakes, zooms).
- [ ] Captions match the voice word for word and change with it.
- [ ] No darkened or blurred patches; no leftover old captions or watermarks.
- [ ] Readable on a phone: captions inside the safe zone (clear of the
      TikTok and Reels buttons and caption area), large enough to read at
      arm's length, on screen long enough to read twice.
- [ ] Works with the sound off (captions carry the message).
- [ ] No identifiable under-18 without a parent's written consent; app
      screens from the tester account.
- [ ] Ends on one clear action that matches the activation goal (try the
      demo, log a first session).
- [ ] Watched once more start to finish at real speed on a phone-sized
      window.
