## Risk found in the code, not reported (for builder and qa-tester)

- Safari's WebCodecs `VideoDecoder` gave frames out of order on B-frame clips until a fix in Jan 2026
  ([WebKit 287516](https://bugs.webkit.org/show_bug.cgi?id=287516)). `src/lib/video/clipDecoder.ts:154`
  draws frames in the order the decoder gives them. On an iPhone not yet updated, an upload could get
  wrong makes and misses (wrong data). Not seen yet. Cheap guard: skip a frame whose time goes backwards
  and report it once. Phone check: upload an iPhone clip on an iPhone and compare the calls with the
  clip. If a player reports wrong calls after an upload on an iPhone, this is the first suspect.
